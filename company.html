Acquisition strategy

How we get clients for them

Shift share from rented aggregator leads to owned, tracked demand — without pretending to be ElectroMech scale.

Channels we turn on

  • P0 LinkedIn — company + MD · first 8 posts · WhatsApp CTA
  • Google Business / Maps — Local Pack for Bangalore terms
  • City money-pages — EOT manufacturers in Bangalore / Karnataka
  • Product RFQ + WA — capture intent on-page (vs Konecranes friction)
  • Price guide — 5 T / 10 T search demand premium brands ignore
  • IndiaMART kept — but UTM’d into CRM (measure, don’t worship)

What “winning” looks like

  • Not 10,000 installs overnight
  • Yes shortlist for every serious BLR EOT RFQ
  • Yes growing owned RFQ share vs IndiaMART
  • Yes AMC renewals tracked as recurring
  • Yes 6+ real case studies from existing clients
  • Yes MD story + factory film as trust layer
