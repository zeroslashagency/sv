01 · Vision

What we are building toward

One sentence the MD can repeat. Everything else is how we get there.

Core thesis

SVMH becomes the trusted Bengaluru crane partner buyers find first online — standards-clear, price-honest, reachable on WhatsApp — not another silent IndiaMART listing.

Not global premium Not price-only MSME Owned leads > rented South India · IS codes

Shift

05 · Website redesign

Exact scope. Nothing vague.

Not “make it pretty.” A commercial system: brand, products, proof, enquire, track.

  • 01Secure rebuild — SSL, fast, mobile-first
  • 02Brand system — Steel Navy / Forge Copper on every page
  • 03Dual-axis nav — Products × Industries
  • 04Money-pages — EOT, gantry, jib, ladle, spares, AMC
  • 05Local pages — Bangalore / Karnataka
  • 06INR price guide — capacity ranges, cost drivers
  • 07RFQ + WhatsApp — on every product page
  • 08Trust strip — ISO PDF, IS codes, GST, MSME
  • 09Maps done right — factory pin, not a random widget
  • 10CRM handoff — form → CRM with UTM source

Out of scope · Phase 1

  • Full ERP / IoT portals
  • 12-language locale site
  • 3D configurator (Phase 4)
  • Fake global claims
  • Carousel hero brand walls

Why not premium-global yet

  • Revenue ~₹13.6 Cr vs ElectroMech ~₹500–700 Cr
  • No catalogue, cases, or cert PDFs online
  • No brand system — old company, thin identity
  • Leads rented; no owned funnel or CRM
  • Site SSL dead — trust floor failed
  • Cannot match Konecranes R&D head-on

Why we still take share

  • Local rivals also weak online — gap is small
  • Premium brands hide ₹ prices — we publish
  • Their RFQ is painful — we do WhatsApp + on-page
  • India IS-code content is thin — open gap
  • Foundry / ladle SERP China-heavy — niche fit
  • Real factory + MD access = MSME trust
