Every page, its target query, funnel stage, template, snippet target and phase. Sources: `../../research/16_Reports/04_SEO_Research_Report.md`, `../../research/12_Content_Ideas/content_plan.md`.

**Confidence convention inherited from the research:** keyword *existence and intent* is High (observed in 12 live SERP pulls, 2026-07-05). Search-*volume magnitude* is Low — validate in Keyword Planner or Ahrefs before spending budget.

## 1. The three gaps we are buying

1. **Price intent** — "EOT crane price 5 ton", "gantry crane price India". Premium brands publish nothing; only marketplaces and a few SEO-savvy makers fill it. An authoritative INR band table is a fast win.
2. **India-standards content** — IS 807 / IS 3177 / IS 4137 results are thin. US hubs (Mazzella, Crane1) own generic technical queries; nobody owns the Indian standards. **No manufacturer consistently wins paragraph or table snippets for technical queries in India.**
3. **Local + service intent** — "eot crane manufacturers in bangalore/karnataka", "crane amc service bangalore" are low-competition and high-intent, and they map straight to the Local Pack.

Plus the fourth wedge that is ours specifically: **foundry / ladle / hot-metal**, where Indian results are thin and China-dominated (Weihua, DGCrane) and which matches SVMH's stated capability.

## 5. Phase 3 — Head terms (Months 9–18+)

`eot crane manufacturer (in india)` · `overhead crane manufacturer` · `top 10 eot crane manufacturers in india` (earned via outreach, not on-page) · industry verticals (`crane for automotive / steel / power / foundry / cement`).

## 7. Internal linking rules

- Pillar ↔ spoke ↔ educational ↔ local, all four directions.
- Every product page links to its standards article, its nearest comparison article, and its own RFQ.
- Product ↔ service ↔ spares triangle on every product page.
- Industry pages act as the lateral connector between products and case studies.
- No educational article ends without a money-page handoff.

## 8. Off-page (not a build task, but the plan needs it)

Earn "top 10" listicle placements (the Pioneer/Loadmate pattern), industry-directory citations with a consistent NAP string, trade-expo mentions (Crane Expo Mumbai, India Steel Expo), and NAP-consistent local citations to feed the Local Pack. Keep the IndiaMART and Justdial profiles alive but point them at the owned site — the goal is to stop *depending* on aggregators, not to abandon the traffic.

## 9. Technical SEO baseline

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| HTTPS | Valid cert, forced redirect, HSTS. **Gate 0 blocker** |
| Meta | Unique title + description on every page (the v1 pages have none) |
| Schema | Organization, LocalBusiness (`areaServed`), Product, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, Article |
| Sitemap / robots | Generated, submitted to Search Console |
| Core Web Vitals | LCP < 2.5s mobile — hence no hero carousel; images as WebP with explicit dimensions |
| Canonicals | Self-referencing; pillar/spoke never compete for the same query |
| Alt text | Spec-bearing: capacity, span, industry |
| GBP | Claimed, categorised, NAP-consistent, photos, Q&A seeded |
