Who we are

S.V. Material Handling System Private Limited manufactures EOT cranes, gantry cranes, jib cranes, and wire-rope hoists at our works in Harohalli KIADB Industrial Area, Bengaluru.

We have been in business since 2006, serving factories, warehouses, foundries, and steel plants across Karnataka and South India. Our cranes are designed to IS 807, IS 3177, and IS 4137 standards, and manufactured under ISO 9001:2015 quality management.

SVMH is led by Managing Director Shri D. Umapathi. We are a family-owned business with an in-house design team, fabrication facility, and service network. Every crane is engineered to order and backed by AMC and spare parts support.

We supply single girder and double girder EOT cranes from 1 tonne to 50 tonne capacity, hot metal and ladle cranes for foundries, gantry cranes for outdoor yards, and jib cranes for workstation handling. We also service and modernize cranes of any make.
