When to choose gantry

No overhead runway, or the bay needs to stay open

Precast yards, steel stockyards, assembly areas, shipyards, and anywhere a building column would block the floor. The crane walks on ground rails instead of hanging from the roof structure.

System types

Three gantry configurations, each for a different site

01

Double-leg goliath

Portal frame with legs both sides. Crane travels the full yard length. For precast yards, steel stockyards, container handling, shipyards.

5 – 100+ T · wide span · outdoor

02

Single-leg gantry

One leg on ground rail, one side on building runway. For retrofit situations where one wall already has a runway beam but the opposite side does not.

5 – 50 T · asymmetric · retrofit

03

Semi-gantry

One side elevated on a runway, other side on ground rail, with cantilever overhang. For narrow bays where floor space below is critical.

2 – 30 T · cantilever · space-saving

Outdoor duty design

Wind, rain and thermal expansion change the specification

An outdoor gantry is not an EOT crane moved outside. Wind load per IS 875 Part 3 affects leg design and wheel loading. Rail clamps prevent storm drift. The coating system matches the site environment — marine, industrial or general outdoor — instead of a workshop paint schedule.

Temperature swings expand the span. Rail expansion joints and wheel-flange clearances are calculated, not assumed. If the crane sits outdoors year-round in Bengaluru, the ambient range is roughly 15–38°C; in Rajasthan or coastal sites the range is wider and the design follows.
