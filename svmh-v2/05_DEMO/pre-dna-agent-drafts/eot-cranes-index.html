What we make

Cranes specified from your load cycle, not a catalogue page

Duty class derived from lifts per day and average load per IS 3177. Girder analysed to IS 807 deflection limits. Hoist and drive makes named in the technical offer, not left as "equivalent".

Product types

Three EOT configurations, each for a different job

01

Double-girder EOT

Crab on top of two girders. Higher hook, auxiliary hoist, cabin or radio control, walkway access. For spans above 15 m and duty above M6.

5 – 100+ T · up to 32 m · M5 – M8

02

Single-girder EOT

One box girder, underslung hoist on the lower flange. The right answer for workshops and light fabrication, and roughly 30–45% cheaper than double girder at equal capacity.

0.5 – 15 T · ≤ 30 m · M3 – M6

03

Ladle & hot metal

IS 4137 design basis. A load that cannot be set down and cannot be dropped changes the rope, brakes, girder and inspection regime. 8:1 rope factor, redundant braking.

IS 4137 · M7 – M8 · 8 : 1 rope

Industry applications

Where EOT cranes work hardest

Automotive & general manufacturing

Press shop, body transfer, machine tending. Cycle time dominates, so VFD control and anti-sway matter more than headline capacity.

M5 – M6 · VFD · radio

Steel & metal processing

Coil, billet and slab handling. Continuous severe service — structure and running gear sized for fatigue rather than peak lift.

M7 – M8 · heavy double

Foundry & hot metal

Pour-cycle-driven design under IS 4137. The capability we lead with. Redundant braking, heat shielding, monthly inspection.

IS 4137 · M8

Power & energy

Turbine-hall rotor and transformer lifts. Precision creep speeds, generous hook height, maintenance access designed in.

Heavy double · M6+

Cement & construction materials

Moulds, batch-plant components. Dust ingress and outdoor exposure drive enclosure rating and coating specification.

Dust-proofed · coated

Heavy fabrication & EPC

Structural handling across long spans. Special service conditions get calculated rather than assumed away.

Long span · heavy
