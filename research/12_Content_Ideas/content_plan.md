**Method:** Grounded in this repo's prior research (`03_SEO_Research/seo_research.md`, `01_Competitors`, `02_Website_Audits`, `06_Industry_Research`, `08_Brochures`, `09_Case_Studies`, `10_Certifications`) + fresh live SERP/competitor verification via firecrawl_search on 2026-07-05.

## 0. How to read this plan

Every idea carries five fields, as required:
- **Target keyword / intent** — the primary query + search intent (Commercial / Commercial-Local / Transactional / Investigation / Informational).
- **Format** — page type (money-page, pillar, blog/guide, comparison, FAQ, glossary, calculator handoff, case study, etc.).
- **Funnel stage** — TOFU (awareness) / MOFU (consideration) / BOFU (decision).
- **Why it wins** — the specific competitive/SEO reason, tied to evidence.
- **Evidence** — source URL(s), date accessed, confidence, supporting snippet.

**Confidence convention:** Confidence on *keyword existence & intent* is High (observed in live SERPs, per `seo_research.md`). Confidence on *search-volume magnitude* is Low (no licensed volume tool used) — validate in Google Keyword Planner / Ahrefs before budget allocation. This convention inherits directly from `03_SEO_Research/seo_research.md` §1.

**Strategic spine (from the evidence):** SVMH is a ~₹13.6 Cr family MSME with weak digital presence competing in a SERP dominated by (a) marketplace aggregators and (b) SEO-savvy manufacturers. The four defensible content wedges the research proves are open:
1. **Bengaluru/Karnataka local content** — national brands under-invest locally; local rivals (Associated Hoists, ABCO, Gayathri, Ace, Peenya fabricators) are beatable. (`seo_research.md` §1.2, §9.1)
2. **INR price-transparency content** — premium brands avoid it. (`seo_research.md` §0, §9.2)
3. **India-standards education (IS 807 / IS 3177 / IS 4137)** — a genuine featured-snippet gap; US/China hubs own FEM/CMAA but not Indian standards. (`seo_research.md` §7, §9.3)
4. **Foundry / ladle / hot-metal crane content** — India results thin and China-dominated, and it matches SVMH's stated niche. (`seo_research.md` §1.8, §9.4)

## 13. Content standards (apply to every page — so it actually ranks)

Derived from the Konecranes SEO audit (`02_Website_Audits/01_Konecranes_audit.md` §11, §16.4) and `seo_research.md` §3, §7:
1. **One H1 per page**, unique templated title + benefit-rich meta description.
2. **Snippet-first structure:** lead each target section with a direct 40–55 word answer; H2 phrased as the question; `<table>` for comparisons; `<ol>` for processes.
3. **Schema:** Organization + Product/Offer (price tables) + BreadcrumbList + FAQPage + HowTo (guides).
4. **Descriptive keyword-rich internal anchors** (blog→money-page handoff per `seo_research.md` §5.2); breadcrumbs on every page.
5. **Real media:** original product/factory photos with descriptive alt text; lightweight video that plays on click (beat Konecranes' cookie-gated video — `01_Konecranes_audit.md` §16 AVOID #2).
6. **Inline low-friction conversion** on every product page: short RFQ (product pre-filled) + WhatsApp/click-to-call — directly beating Konecranes' RFQ friction (`01_Konecranes_audit.md` §7 Read).
7. **Fast, mobile-first build** (Indian mobile network conditions) — a concrete wedge over heavy incumbents (`01_Konecranes_audit.md` §13–14).
