**Client:** S.V. Material Handling System Pvt Ltd (SVMH), Bengaluru — svind.co.in
**Scope:** Keyword landscape, competitor rankings, SERP features, content clusters, internal-linking strategy, FAQ + featured-snippet opportunities, and a prioritized keyword target list for SVMH.
**Date accessed for all live SERP observations:** 2026-07-05
**Method:** Live SERP observation via firecrawl_search / firecrawl_scrape (Google-backed results). Search-volume figures are NOT from a paid keyword tool — they are labeled as qualitative estimates (Confidence: Low) unless otherwise noted. Rankings reflect a single point-in-time, un-localized pull and will vary by user geo/personalization.

## 0. Executive Summary

- **The India crane SERP is dominated by two page types:** (a) B2B marketplace aggregators (IndiaMART `dir.indiamart.com`, TradeIndia, ExportersIndia, Justdial, Aajjo) and (b) manufacturer money-pages optimized for `[product] + manufacturer/supplier + India/[city]`. SVMH currently leans on the former (IndiaMART/ExportersIndia), which caps its brand equity and margin.
- **"Manufacturer" / "supplier" / "in India" / "in [city]" is the core commercial modifier.** Nearly every ranking manufacturer page targets the pattern `<crane type> manufacturer[s] in India` or `... in <city>`. This is the single most important on-page pattern to replicate.
- **Price/cost intent is huge and under-served by premium brands.** Queries like "gantry crane price India", "EOT crane price 5 ton", "10 ton goliath crane price in India 2026" pull marketplace listings and a few SEO-savvy manufacturers (SBE Crane, Gearcon, Loadmate). Top brands (Konecranes, ElectroMech) largely ignore price content — a clear gap SVMH can own.
- **"Top 10 / best manufacturer" listicles are a ranking and link-building battleground.** Pioneer Cranes, Loadmate, HYCrane, KSCrane, MachanX all rank listicles that shape buyer shortlists. SVMH is absent from these.
- **Informational/technical content (single vs double girder, duty class M5/M7, crane cost factors) is won by educational hubs** (Mazzella, Crane1, Konecranes, Demag, R&M, Indef). This is the top-of-funnel cluster SVMH needs to build authority and win featured snippets.
- **SERP features present:** People Also Ask (PAA), video carousels (YouTube), image packs, marketplace price-list rich results, Local Pack / Google Business (for "[city]" queries), and listicle featured snippets. No manufacturer is consistently winning paragraph/table snippets for technical queries in India — open opportunity.

## 5. Internal-Linking Strategy

1. **Pillar ↔ Spoke bidirectional links.** Every product money-page links up to its pillar hub and sideways to sibling products (e.g., Single Girder EOT ↔ Double Girder EOT ↔ Underslung). Pillar hub links down to all spokes.
2. **Blog → Product (commercial handoff).** Every educational article links to the relevant product/service money-page with descriptive anchor text (e.g., "single vs double girder" article → "Double Girder EOT Crane" page). This passes topical authority to money-pages and captures informational readers mid-funnel.
3. **Product → Service → Spares triangle.** Crane product pages link to AMC/inspection services and to relevant spares (e.g., Double Girder EOT → Crane AMC + DSL Busbar + Forged Hook), maximizing per-visit page depth and cross-sell.
4. **Industry pages as connectors.** Each industry vertical page links to the specific crane types it uses (Foundry → Ladle/Hot Metal Crane; Automotive → Single Girder EOT + Jib) — mirrors ElectroMech's high-performing industry IA.
5. **Local pages → product + service.** "Crane Manufacturer in Bangalore" links to all product pillars + AMC service, consolidating local relevance.
6. **Descriptive, keyword-rich anchors** (not "click here"). Use exact/partial-match anchors that mirror target keywords.
7. **Breadcrumbs** (Home > EOT Cranes > Double Girder) with BreadcrumbList schema for SERP breadcrumb display.
8. **Contextual "Related" modules** at page bottom (Related products / Related guides) to distribute link equity and reduce bounce.
