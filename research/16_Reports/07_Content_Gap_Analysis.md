## Executive Summary

SVMH competes in the India overhead/EOT crane market (~USD 192.8 Mn in 2025, ~6.31% CAGR to USD 339.8 Mn by 2034 — IMARC) as a ~₹13.6 Cr family MSME whose entire digital presence is a routing node to IndiaMART/ExportersIndia. Against that backdrop, **content is not a marketing nicety for SVMH — it is the single lever that can move the company off rented aggregator traffic and onto owned, higher-margin demand.** This report consolidates the content and SEO gaps identified across the research corpus into one prioritized roadmap, organized by funnel stage (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU) and content cluster.

The core finding is stark and was confirmed live on 2026-07-05: **SVMH has effectively zero content assets** — no product money-pages, no downloadable catalogue or datasheet, no blog, no case studies, no educational or standards content, no local landing pages, and (as of this audit) an **expired SSL certificate on svind.co.in that prevents the site from loading securely at all.** Meanwhile the competitive field is stratified. At the top, ElectroMech runs a full content engine: a dual-axis product × industry IA with **13 industry landing pages** (verified live), a **10+ article technical/buyer blog** (verified live), ~25 case studies, and a resource hub of ungated brochures. In the middle, SEO-savvy regional players (Timeskrane, Loadmate, Pioneer, K2 Cranes) own price guides, "top 10" listicles, and multi-city pages. Even sub-scale rivals (S. Cranes, Vikrant, Top Crane, Shubhlaxmi) publish a downloadable PDF catalogue. But directly beside SVMH in Bengaluru, rivals like **Associated Hoists have no blog, no guides, no case studies, no downloads, and no pricing** (verified live) — proving the local field is beatable.

Five content wedges are open and defensible for SVMH, in priority order:

1. **Bengaluru/Karnataka local content** — national brands under-invest locally; local rivals publish product listings only. Several Karnataka long-tails are uncontested. This is the highest-priority, lowest-competition, highest-intent cluster.
2. **INR price-transparency content** — premium brands (Konecranes, ElectroMech) refuse to publish prices; Timeskrane and a few others own this by default. A schema-marked INR price/spec table cluster captures high-intent transactional search.
3. **India-standards education (IS 807 / IS 3177 / IS 4137 / Factories Act 1948)** — a genuine featured-snippet gap; US/China hubs own FEM/CMAA but not Indian standards.
4. **Foundry / ladle / hot-metal crane content** — India results are thin and China-dominated (Weihua, DGCrane); this matches SVMH's stated hot/ladle niche and is higher-margin.
5. **Service/AMC + engineering resources (calculators, CAD, checklists)** — recurring-revenue keywords are poorly served by manufacturers, and interactive tools/CAD libraries are almost absent among India peers.

The recommended sequence publishes **revenue-carrying money-pages and local pages first** (Phase 1, months 0–4), then the industry and product pages that broaden coverage (Phase 2, months 3–9), then the educational/standards/comparison content that passes topical authority into those money-pages and wins snippets (Phase 3, months 4–12), and finally the head-term and off-page push (Phase 4, months 9–18+). A prerequisite "Phase 0" fixes the expired certificate and ships basic technical SEO hygiene, without which no content can rank.

The India crane SERP is dominated by two page types: (a) B2B marketplace aggregators (IndiaMART, TradeIndia, ExportersIndia, Justdial, Aajjo) and (b) manufacturer money-pages built on the `<crane type> manufacturer in <India/city>` pattern (`03_SEO_Research` §0, High). | Foundry/ladle/hot-metal | Weihua, DGCrane, Anupam (JSW 550T); **India thin** | Absent | **Medium-High (niche match)** |
| Service/AMC | MegaCranes, MHC, CraneCare, Cranedge, K2 | Absent | Medium-High (margin) |

**The trap (verified across the corpus):** SVMH's reliance on IndiaMART/ExportersIndia means renting traffic and competing on price alongside 200+ listings on nearly every commercial and local query. Every content asset built on the owned domain reduces that dependency. As the SEO research puts it: aggregators "out-rank most individual manufacturers on generic terms because of domain authority + fresh price listings" (`03_SEO_Research` §2.3, High).

## 5. Prioritized Content Roadmap

Prioritization logic (inherited from `03_SEO_Research` §8): **(Relevance to SVMH product line) × (Local advantage as Bengaluru maker) × (Achievability vs entrenched brands/aggregators) × (Commercial value).** As a small MSME with weak current presence, SVMH starts with defensible local + long-tail + service terms, then expands to head terms as authority builds.

### Phase 0 — Prerequisites (Immediate, Week 0–2)

Non-negotiable before any content ships:
1. **Fix the expired SSL certificate on svind.co.in** — the site currently fails to load securely (verified live 2026-07-05). No page can rank or convert while this is broken. Highest-urgency item in the entire roadmap.
2. **Technical SEO baseline** — one H1/page, unique templated title + meta, self-referencing canonical, Organization + Product + BreadcrumbList JSON-LD, a real XML sitemap + robots.txt with honest lastmod. Cheap; Konecranes provides the exact template (`02_Website_Audits/01_Konecranes_audit.md` §11, §16.4, High).
3. **Google Business Profile** fully optimized (category, photos, reviews, service areas) to compete in the Local Pack (`03_SEO_Research` §3, High).

### Phase 1 — Foundation & Quick Wins (Months 0–4)

Publish: **L1, L2, L5** (local); **P1, P2, P8** (core products + price tables); **B2** (INR price guide); **F1, F4** (FAQ); **D9 + D1–D6** (catalogue + core datasheets); **SF1, SF6** (ISO 9001 cert + signed policy).

Rationale: Local + long-tail + price = defensible, high-intent, low-competition (`03_SEO_Research` Tier 1). Datasheets/catalogue close the parity gap that puts SVMH below tiny rivals. This phase carries the most revenue per unit of effort.

### Phase 2 — Core Commercial (Months 3–9)

Publish: **P3–P7** (remaining products); **L3, L4, L6** (Karnataka/city); **V1–V3** (auto/steel/foundry); **P4 + S3** (ladle crane + IS 4137 — the niche wedge); **CS1–CS2** (first case studies); **D8, D10** (RFQ template + vertical one-pagers); **M1–M3** (inspection checklists).

Rationale: Product money-pages + industry pages + the foundry/ladle wedge and first proof assets (`03_SEO_Research` Tier 2). Defends the Karnataka home turf against K2 Cranes.

### Phase 3 — Authority & Top-of-Funnel (Months 4–12)

Publish: **B3, B4, B5, B6** (guides); **C1–C5** (comparisons); **S1, S2, S4, S5** (standards); **G1–G3** (glossary); **F2, F3, F5**; **V4–V6** (remaining verticals); **T1, T4, T6** (calculators); **CAD1–CAD4** (core CAD library).

Rationale: Educational + snippet capture that passes topical authority into money-pages (`03_SEO_Research` Tier 3, §5). Owns the India IS-standards snippet gap and the calculator/CAD leapfrog.

### Phase 4 — Head Terms & Off-page (Months 9–18+)

## 6. Content Standards (Apply to Every Page)

Derived from the Konecranes audit (`02_Website_Audits/01_Konecranes_audit.md` §11, §16) and `03_SEO_Research` §3, §7:

1. **One H1 per page**, unique templated title + benefit-rich meta description.
2. **Snippet-first structure** — lead each target section with a direct 40–55 word answer; H2 phrased as the question; `<table>` for comparisons; `<ol>` for processes.
3. **Schema** — Organization + Product/Offer (price tables) + BreadcrumbList + FAQPage + HowTo (guides/checklists).
4. **Internal linking** — pillar↔spoke bidirectional; blog→money-page handoff with keyword-rich anchors; product→service→spares triangle; industry pages as connectors; breadcrumbs on every page (`03_SEO_Research` §5).
5. **Inline low-friction conversion** on every product page — short RFQ (product pre-filled) + WhatsApp/click-to-call, directly beating Konecranes' RFQ friction (`02_Website_Audits/01_Konecranes_audit.md` §7).
6. **Real media** — original product/factory photos with descriptive alt text; lightweight video that plays on click (beat Konecranes' cookie-gated video).
7. **Fast, mobile-first build** — a concrete wedge over heavy incumbents on Indian mobile networks (`02_Website_Audits/01_Konecranes_audit.md` §13–14).
8. **Accuracy + disclaimer** — every calculator/checklist/load figure shows its formula/assumptions and the competent-person disclaimer; verify all IS revision numbers against the live BIS catalogue before publishing (`06_Industry_Research` §11; `15_Engineering_Resources` §0).

## 7. Key Risks & Caveats

- **Search-volume magnitude is Low-confidence** across the corpus — no licensed volume tool was used. Validate every target keyword in Google Keyword Planner / Ahrefs before allocating budget. Keyword *existence and intent* are High-confidence (live SERP observation).
- **IS standard revision numbers must be verified against the live BIS catalogue** before any compliance claim is published (IS 3177:2020 vs 1999; adjacent IS 15419/13834 uncertain — `06_Industry_Research` §11, §8).
- **Engineering resources carry liability** — calculators, checklists, and load charts are decision-support aids, not certified approvals. The competent-person disclaimer is mandatory (`15_Engineering_Resources` §0).
- **Pricing figures are vendor-published indicative ranges** (Medium confidence), cross-referenced across ≥2 sources; actual project pricing varies widely with span/lift/duty/civil scope. Present as ranges, not fixed prices.
- **SERP rankings are point-in-time, un-localized pulls** (High confidence for presence, Medium for exact position due to personalization/geo).
- **The SSL/certificate issue on svind.co.in** was observed live on 2026-07-05 and blocks the entire strategy until fixed — treat as an urgent prerequisite, not a content item.
