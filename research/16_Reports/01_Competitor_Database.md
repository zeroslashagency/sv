**Prepared for:** S.V. Material Handling System Pvt Ltd (SVMH), Bengaluru · **Date:** 2026-07-05
**Sources:** `01_Competitors/` (index + 14 deep profiles), `02_Website_Audits/`, `06_Industry_Research/`.

## Executive Summary
The industrial-crane competitive set spans **71 identified companies across 8 tiers** — from ~USD 3B global leaders (Konecranes, which owns Demag + WMI) to same-street Bengaluru fabricators. SVMH (~₹13.6 Cr) sits in the **MSME regional tier**, but its *product portfolio* (EOT, gantry, jib, monorail, hot/ladle, spares, service) overlaps players 5–50× its size. The database below ranks competitors by **threat level to SVMH**, blending portfolio overlap, geographic proximity, and digital strength. The most acute threats are not the global giants (different buyer tier) but **digitally-savvy South-India regionals** (K2 Cranes, Amsak, Mega Cranes) and **same-city Bengaluru rivals** (Pegasus, Kiran, ABCO, Associated Hoists) who compete for the exact same local factory orders — and increasingly win them online, where SVMH is nearly invisible.

## 3. Key structural findings
- **Consolidation is accelerating:** Columbus McKinnon acquired Kito Crosby (Feb 2026, ~USD 2.7B); Konecranes owns Demag + WMI; Kito India acquired Eepos (2024). The top of the market is concentrating.
- **The barbell:** a few engineering-led/MNC players at the top vs hundreds of MSME fabricators (SVMH's tier) competing on price via IndiaMART/ExportersIndia.
- **SVMH's real battleground is South India + online.** The global giants don't chase ₹10–35L local EOT jobs; the regionals do — and they're winning discovery through Justdial/IndiaMART/SEO that SVMH lacks.
