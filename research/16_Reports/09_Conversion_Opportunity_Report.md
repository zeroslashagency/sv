**Prepared for:** SVMH, Bengaluru · **Date:** 2026-07-05
**Sources:** `14_Conversion_Ideas/`, `13_Feature_Ideas/`, `05_Customer_Research/`, website audits.

## Executive Summary
Competitors leak conversions in three predictable ways: **(1) they make buyers leave the product page to enquire, (2) they hide price and specs, (3) their heroes dilute the CTA.** SVMH's conversion edge is to remove friction at the moment of intent, mirror the real **IS 3177 RFQ mental model**, and lead with proof. The single highest-ROI move is an **on-page smart RFQ (product pre-selected) + sticky WhatsApp/call** — something no audited competitor offers frictionlessly. This report consolidates the CRO plan.

## 2. Recommended RFQ flow
1. **Entry:** on-page "Request a Quote" (product pre-selected) or WhatsApp.
2. **Qualify (IS 3177):** crane type → capacity → span → lift height → duty/usage → indoor/outdoor/environment.
3. **Contact:** name, company, phone, email, city (minimal, after value shown).
4. **Fast-track:** instant WhatsApp/callback; "typical response within X hours."
5. **Route:** spec'd/hot → sales; incomplete → nurture (buyer guide + spec-sheet email).
This doubles as **buyer education + lead qualification**, matching how buyers actually define requirements.

## 3. Trust signals (priority order)
1. Industry-tagged reference installs + FAT/factory video (biggest lever per customer intel).
2. Certifications (ISO 9001, IS 807/3177 declaration, GST, MSME) + downloadable.
3. Years/installs/capacity stats.
4. Named client logos + case studies.
5. Local service coverage map + guaranteed response time.
6. Spares stocking policy (the maintenance-engineer gatekeeper decides on this).

## 5. Expected outcome
Removing RFQ friction + adding WhatsApp + proof-first layout targets the top-of-page conversion the entire competitive set neglects. Combined with transparent price guidance (which pre-qualifies budget) and lead-magnet downloads (which convert researching engineers), SVMH can convert a materially higher share of the same traffic than the price-opaque, friction-heavy incumbents.
