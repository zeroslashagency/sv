# SEO Research Report — Keyword Strategy & Content Architecture (SVMH Phase 1)

**Prepared for:** SVMH, Bengaluru · **Date:** 2026-07-05
**Sources:** `03_SEO_Research/seo_research.md` (12 live SERP pulls), `12_Content_Ideas/`, `11_Market_Gaps/`.

## Executive Summary
SVMH enters an SEO landscape with **three wide-open gaps**: (1) **price/cost intent** ("EOT crane price 5 ton", "gantry crane price India") that premium brands ignore and only a few SEO-savvy makers touch; (2) **India-specific technical/standards content** (IS 807/3177/4137, single-vs-double girder, duty class) where no manufacturer consistently wins snippets; and (3) **local + service intent** ("EOT crane manufacturers in Bangalore", "crane AMC service Karnataka") that is thin and highly winnable. The strategy is a **hub-and-spoke architecture** with a phased keyword plan: start with defensible local + long-tail + service terms (Months 0–4), build product money-pages (3–9), then authority/educational content (4–12) before contesting head terms (9–18+).

## 1. The three SEO gaps to own
1. **Price gap** — head brands (Konecranes, ElectroMech) publish no price content; marketplace listings + a few makers (Gearcon, Loadmate, SBE) fill it. Authoritative INR price tables = quick wins.
2. **Standards/technical gap** — IS 807/IS 3177/IS 4137 content is "thin in top results"; US hubs (Mazzella, Crane1) own generic technical queries. India-specific angle is open.
3. **Local/service gap** — "eot crane manufacturers in karnataka/bangalore", "crane amc service bangalore" are low-competition, high-intent, and map to Local Pack/GBP.

## 2. SERP features present (to target)
People Also Ask (PAA), video carousels (YouTube), image packs, marketplace price-list rich results, Local Pack/GBP (for city queries), and listicle featured snippets. **No manufacturer consistently wins paragraph/table snippets for technical queries in India** — open.

## 3. Content cluster architecture (hub-and-spoke)
- **Pillar 1 — EOT/Overhead** (`/eot-cranes`): single-girder, double-girder, underslung/monorail, hot/ladle/foundry (India gap) + blog spokes.
- **Pillar 2 — Gantry/Goliath** (`/gantry-cranes`): single, double-goliath, semi-goliath, portable + blog.
- **Pillar 3 — Jib/Slewing** (`/jib-cranes`): pillar/wall/360° + guide.
- **Pillar 4 — Hoists** (`/hoists`): wire-rope, chain, crab unit + duty guide.
- **Pillar 5 — Components & Spares** (`/crane-spare-parts`): DSL busbar, forged hooks, rope drums, sheaves, wheels, gearboxes, pendants.
- **Pillar 6 — Services** (`/services`): AMC/PM, inspection/load-testing, modernization, fabrication, operator training.
- **Local cluster** (`/locations`) + **standards cluster** (IS 807/3177/4137 explained).
All cross-linked pillar → spoke → educational/local (proven by ElectroMech IA + Mazzella learning center).

## 4. Prioritized keyword plan (phased)
**Tier 1 — Quick wins (Months 0–4, local + long-tail, low comp, high intent):**
`eot crane manufacturers in bangalore` · `overhead crane supplier bangalore` · `eot crane manufacturer in karnataka` · `gantry crane manufacturer bangalore` · `crane amc service bangalore/karnataka` · `5 ton eot crane price` · `dsl busbar manufacturer` · `crane spare parts bangalore`.

**Tier 2 — Core commercial (Months 3–9, product money-pages):**
`double/single girder eot crane manufacturer` · `gantry/goliath crane manufacturer` · `jib crane manufacturer india` · `wire rope hoist manufacturer` · `monorail/underslung crane manufacturer` · `foundry/ladle crane manufacturer india` (India-vs-China gap) · `crane spare parts supplier india`.

**Tier 3 — Authority/TOFU (Months 4–12, educational + snippets):**
`single girder vs double girder crane` · `what is eot crane / eot full form` · `crane duty class / FEM 9.511 / M5 M7 / IS 807 classification` · `eot crane price in india / how much does an overhead crane cost` · `crane amc — what's included`.

**Tier 4 — Head terms (Months 9–18+, brand-building):**
`eot crane manufacturer (in india)` · `overhead crane manufacturer` · `top 10 eot crane manufacturers in india` (earn via outreach) · industry verticals (`crane for automotive/steel/power/foundry/cement`).

## 5. Featured-snippet targets (winnable now)
what is an eot crane (paragraph) · single vs double girder (table) · crane duty class M5/M7 (table) · **IS 807 classification (India gap)** · eot crane price in india (table) · gantry vs goliath vs semi-goliath (table) · crane amc — what's included (list) · jib crane types (list). Tactics: 40–55-word lead answer, `<table>` for comparisons, `<ol>` for processes, question-phrased H2, FAQPage/HowTo schema.

## 6. Internal linking & off-page
- **Internal:** pillar ↔ spoke ↔ educational ↔ local; every product page links to its standards, comparison, and RFQ.
- **Off-page:** earn "top 10" listicle placements (Pioneer/Loadmate style), industry-directory citations, trade-expo mentions (Crane Expo Mumbai, India Steel Expo), NAP-consistent local citations for Local Pack; maintain IndiaMART/Justdial profiles but drive to the owned site.

## 7. Quick-win vs long-game
- **0–4 months:** local money-pages + GBP + price tables + service pages → fast, defensible traffic.
- **4–12 months:** product money-pages + educational hub + snippets → authority.
- **9–18+ months:** head terms + listicle placements → brand-scale visibility.
