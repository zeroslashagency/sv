# Market Research Report — Indian Industrial Crane Sector (SVMH Phase 1)

**Prepared for:** SVMH, Bengaluru · **Date:** 2026-07-05
**Sources:** `06_Industry_Research/`, `05_Customer_Research/`, `04_Product_Research/`, competitor profiles.

## Executive Summary
The India overhead/EOT crane market is a **~USD 180–195 Mn (2024–25) segment growing ~6.0–6.3% CAGR** toward ~USD 340 Mn by 2034 — a niche inside the ~USD 3.76 Bn India crane market and ~USD 11.4 Bn India MHE market. Growth rests on three durable engines: the **National Steel Policy** (300 MTPA by 2030-31), sustained **infrastructure capex** (Make in India, Smart Cities, Bharatmala, metros/airports), and **PLI-driven manufacturing** in auto, electronics, and capital goods. The market is **barbelled** — a few engineering-led/MNC players at the top, hundreds of MSME fabricators (SVMH's tier) below, competing on price via aggregators. The strategic prize: the whole segment is shifting toward **smart/IoT, higher-duty, safety-certified cranes evaluated on total cost of ownership**, yet most MSME competitors have weak digital presence. A standards-forward, technically credible website lets SVMH punch above its ₹13.6 Cr weight.

## 2. Demand drivers
- **Steel:** National Steel Policy → 300 MTPA by 2030-31; every new mill/foundry needs EOT, ladle, and process cranes (M7–M8).
- **Infrastructure capex:** Bharatmala, metros, airports, Smart Cities → structural fabrication, precast, gantry demand.
- **PLI manufacturing:** auto, electronics, capital goods capacity additions → single/double-girder EOT + jib.
- **Ports/logistics:** container and cargo handling → gantry/RTG/RMG.
- **Replacement + compliance:** ageing crane fleets + tightening factory-safety enforcement (IS 807) drive upgrades.

## 3. Segmentation (IMARC)
- **Type:** Gantry / Jib / Bridge (EOT) / Others. **Capacity:** ≤5T / 6–10T / 11–50T / >50T. **End-use:** Manufacturing, Auto & Transport, Construction, Metal & Mining, Power & Utilities, Ports & Shipyards. **Region:** N/S/E/W India.
- **SVMH sweet spot:** 5–20 T single/double-girder EOT for South-India manufacturing/auto/steel — the highest-volume segment (5T ₹10–18L, 10T ₹20–35L).

Dynamics buyers care about: GST 18%; single-girder ~30–45% cheaper at equal capacity; 5T→10T jump ~80–100%; duty class adds 20–40%; runway/civil/freight/AMC often *excluded* from headline quotes (a transparency angle SVMH can own).

## 5. Competitive dynamics
- **Consolidation:** Konecranes owns Demag + WMI; Columbus McKinnon acquired Kito Crosby (Feb 2026); Kito India bought Eepos (2024).
- **Tier 1:** Konecranes/WMI, ElectroMech (₹500–697 Cr). **Tier 2/3:** hundreds of MSME fabricators incl. SVMH, discovered via IndiaMART/ExportersIndia.
- **Regional hubs:** Pune/Mumbai (West), **Bengaluru/Coimbatore/Chennai (South — SVMH's base)**, NCR (North), Gujarat (West), Kolkata/Durgapur (East steel belt).

## 6. Trends
- **Smart/IoT cranes** — remote monitoring, predictive maintenance (Konecranes-led; open in India MSME tier).
- **Safety & compliance** — IS 807/3177/4137, FEM duty rating, load-test certification rising as buyer filters.
- **TCO-based buying** — buyers increasingly weigh 20-yr cost over headline price (per customer intel).
- **Digital discovery** — even industrial buyers now research online first; the MSME tier's weak web presence is a structural opening.

## 7. SVMH positioning opportunity
SVMH can differentiate as the **standards-forward, transparent, South-India crane partner**: IS-code-compliant engineering, honest ₹ price guidance, TCO education, strong AMC/spares, and a credible digital presence — capturing the buyers the giants ignore and the regionals can't yet reach online.
