# Certifications: What Competitors Hold & What Buyers Expect

> Inventory of certifications and compliance credentials competitors display, plus the standards Indian crane buyers actually require. Guides how SVMH should present its own ISO 9001 / IS compliance.
> **Date accessed:** 2026-07-05 unless noted. **Client:** SVMH.

## Why this matters for SVMH
SVMH's compliance is listed as "ISO 9001 (claimed/ready), GST, MSME" — but nothing is shown or downloadable on svind.co.in, and it's flagged "Issuer Not Cooperating" by credit-rating agencies. In this category **buyers treat certificates as gating criteria**, and competitors publish downloadable certificate PDFs. Displaying verifiable certs is a trust necessity, not a nice-to-have.

## Takeaways for the SVMH site
1. **ISO 9001 is table-stakes, not a differentiator.** Every tier — from ElectroMech down to tiny regional fabricators like Top Crane and Associated Hoists — displays ISO 9001. SVMH's "ISO 9001 (claimed/ready)" must become a **verifiable, downloadable certificate** or it reads as a red flag next to peers.
2. **Show the actual certificate PDF, not a logo.** ElectroMech and the mid-market both host real cert PDFs. A JPG badge with no document is weaker than competitors.
3. **Lead compliance messaging with IS 807 + IS 3177 (+ IS 4137 for ladle/foundry).** These are the standards buyers and tenders (GeM, SAIL) screen on. SVMH already cites IS 807/IS 3177/FEM 9.511 — surfacing "designed & tested to IS 807 / IS 3177, IS 4137 for mill-duty" directly answers buyer procurement checklists.
4. **Emphasize third-party inspection & load-test certificates per unit.** Factories Act 1948 makes these mandatory; offering documented type-test / load-test certificates with every crane is a concrete trust play.
5. **Opportunity to leapfrog regionally:** most Bangalore/mid-market rivals stop at ISO 9001. Adding ISO 14001 / ISO 45001 (safety) — as ElectroMech does — would position SVMH above its direct regional competitors on the safety dimension buyers care about most.
6. **CE marking matters for export/MNC clients** (Hercules, Anupam RTG). If SVMH targets exports or MNC plants, CE-compliant product lines become a sales lever.
