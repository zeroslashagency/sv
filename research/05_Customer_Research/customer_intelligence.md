# Customer Intelligence — Industrial Crane Buyers in India (SVMH Phase 1)

How industrial crane buyers in India research, decide, and buy — personas, procurement journey, objections, triggers, RFQ requirements, selection criteria, and after-sales expectations. Built from live research on 2026-07-05. Evidence tagged with Source URL / Confidence.

> **Core insight:** An EOT crane is *not* a commodity. Every unit is engineered to a specific span, capacity, duty class, and structure. Buyers who choose on price alone routinely under-spec duty class and suffer premature fatigue failure (e.g. an M3 crane on an 80-lift/day M5 job cracking within 4 years). The winning seller reframes the purchase from "price of steel" to "20-year total cost of ownership + safety liability." *(Source: ganeshengineeringco.wordpress.com buyer guide, High)*

| Industry | Why they buy | Typical crane type / duty |
|---|---|---|
| Steel & metal processing | Furnace charging, coil/billet/ladle handling | Double-girder, ladle/hot-metal, **M7–M8** |

## Buyer personas

### 1. Plant / Works Manager (economic + technical buyer)
- **Goals:** uptime, throughput, worker safety, on-time capacity expansion.
- **Priorities:** reliability, duty-class correctness, minimal downtime.
- **Pain points:** unplanned breakdowns, slow spares, cranes that fail early from under-spec.
- **Info needs:** reference installs in *their* industry, factory-acceptance-test proof, TCO.
- **Decision role:** sponsors/approves; heavily influences final choice.

### 2. Procurement / Purchase Head (economic buyer)
- **Goals:** best value, defensible vendor selection, commercial terms.
- **Priorities:** uniform RFQ comparison, warranty, vendor financial stability, GST/compliance.
- **Pain points:** apples-to-oranges quotes (one quotes VFD, one contactors, one omits runway); opaque pricing.
- **Info needs:** clear technical offer, price breakdown, delivery, payment terms.
- **Decision role:** owns the PO; runs vendor qualification.

### 3. Maintenance Engineer (technical influencer + after-sales gatekeeper)
- **Goals:** serviceability, spare availability, low failure rate.
- **Priorities:** brand of contactors/drives (Siemens/Schneider/ABB/L&T), spares lead time, service response.
- **Pain points:** 48–72 hr+ spares delays forcing downtime; non-OEM installers.
- **Info needs:** spares stocking policy, PM schedule, 24-hr emergency contact.
- **Decision role:** strong veto on serviceability; specifies components.

### 4. MSME Factory Owner (owner-operator, price-sensitive)
- **Goals:** affordable, "good enough," fast.
- **Priorities:** price, quick delivery, local presence, WhatsApp/phone access to decision-maker.
- **Pain points:** limited technical knowledge → vulnerable to under-spec; budget constraints.
- **Info needs:** simple guidance, indicative price, trust signals (GST, years, local refs).
- **Decision role:** sole decision-maker; buys via IndiaMART/Justdial/referral.

### 5. Consulting Engineer / EPC (technical specifier)
- **Goals:** spec compliance, standards conformance, project schedule.
- **Priorities:** IS 807/3177 compliance, structural calcs (STAAD.Pro), test certs, documentation.
- **Pain points:** vendors using thumb-rule sizing instead of verified calculations.
- **Info needs:** design basis, deflection compliance (IS 807 L/750–L/1000), NDT/weld qualification.
- **Decision role:** writes the spec; shortlists qualified vendors.

*Source: ganeshengineeringco.wordpress.com (procurement-manager framing, component brands, engineering team), revacranes.com. Confidence: Medium–High (personas synthesized from buyer-guide evidence + B2B industrial norms).*

## Procurement journey (stage-by-stage)

1. **Trigger / need** — capacity expansion, new line, breakdown/replacement, safety/compliance push, new facility.
2. **Requirement definition** — document SWL, span, hook height, duty class (M1–M8), speeds, power supply, environment (indoor/outdoor, temp, dust, corrosive, coastal). *If duty class unknown, buyer shares lifts/day + avg load %.*
3. **Discovery / shortlisting** — search (Google, IndiaMART, Justdial, TradeIndia), referrals, match by industry references. **This is where SVMH's weak web presence loses the funnel.**
4. **RFQ issuance** — ideally a fixed technical spec sent to 3+ vendors for uniform comparison.
5. **Technical + commercial offers** — buyer expects a *technical* offer (design basis, hoist make, drive rationale, brake calc, panel layout, paint spec), not just price + date.
6. **Vendor evaluation** — factory visit, reference calls, credential/cert check, financial stability.
7. **Negotiation & PO** — TCO comparison, warranty scope, payment terms.
8. **Manufacturing & FAT** — 6–12 wk lead time normal; factory acceptance test (no-load, 100% SWL, 125% overload).
9. **Installation & commissioning** — runway alignment, conductor bar, on-site load test + certificate; OEM team (not sub-contractor) preferred.
10. **After-sales** — operator/maintenance training (IS 13367), AMC, spares, warranty.

*Source: ganeshengineeringco.wordpress.com (6-step process + install/test), revacranes.com (IS 3177 info set). Confidence: High.*

## Objections & recommended responses

| Objection | Recommended response |
|---|---|
| "Competitor X is cheaper." | Reframe to TCO: under-spec'd duty class fails in 3–5 yrs vs 20; show component/duty differences line-by-line. |
| "How do I know you can deliver quality?" | Reference installs in *their* industry + factory visit + FAT (125% overload) + IS 807/ISO 9001 certs. |
| "What if it breaks down?" | Spares stocking policy, 48–72 hr supply commitment, local service centre, 24-hr emergency line, AMC. |
| "Your delivery is slower than others." | Unrealistically fast = stock components not matched to app / skipped QC; 6–12 wks is correct engineering. |
| "You're a small/family firm vs the big brands." | Direct MD access, faster decisions, local presence, custom engineering, competitive TCO. |
| "Can you handle our heavy/hot-metal job?" | Show M7–M8 / ladle capability, 8:1 rope safety factor, redundant brakes, steel-plant references. |

*Source: ganeshengineeringco.wordpress.com (price/delivery/duty mistakes), corpus. Confidence: Medium–High.*

## Purchase triggers
- Capacity expansion / new production line or bay.
- New greenfield facility or shed (often bundled with PEB/fabrication).
- Existing crane failure, fatigue, or obsolescence (replacement).
- Safety incident or compliance/audit requirement (IS 807, factory inspector).
- Process upgrade needing higher capacity, VFD precision, or automation.
- AMC/service dissatisfaction with incumbent → switch.

*Confidence: Medium (industry-standard triggers, corroborated by buyer-guide context).*

## RFQ requirements checklist (IS 3177 Annexure-A)

The buyer must provide / the seller must capture:
- [ ] Capacity — main hoist (T) and auxiliary hoist (T)
- [ ] Crane type — single / double girder / underhung / gantry / hand-operated
- [ ] **Class of duty — M3 / M5 / M7 / M8**
- [ ] Span (centre-to-centre of rails, mm ≈ building width)
- [ ] Location — indoor / outdoor / both
- [ ] Purpose / nature of load (for magnet/grab: material spec & condition)
- [ ] Speeds — hoist / aux / cross-traverse / long-travel (full + creep; VVVF?)
- [ ] Lift — above floor (m) + below floor (m)
- [ ] Control type — fixed pendant / trailing pendant / cabin (open/closed) / radio remote
- [ ] Platform & lighting (double girder) — single/both side; under-bridge/on-platform
- [ ] Site clearances — rail-bottom to obstruction, to floor, rail-centre to wall/column
- [ ] Hook approaches (hook position from rail centreline)
- [ ] Environmental — ambient temp, humidity, altitude, coastal, hot-metal, seismic zone, hazardous zone
- [ ] Special service — wind load, another crane on same gantry
- [ ] Accessories — anti-collision, slings, lifting beams, magnet, grab bucket

*Source: revacranes.com Buyer's Guide (IS 3177 Annexure-A, directly quoted). Confidence: High.*

## Selection criteria (how buyers choose between suppliers)
1. **Track record & installed base** in the buyer's industry (ask: cranes installed last 5 yrs, can I visit a site?).
2. **In-house manufacturing** — structural fab, machining, panel assembly in-house; traceable component sources (red flag if all outsourced).
3. **Engineering team & software** — designs to IS 807, STAAD.Pro girder analysis (not thumb-rule).
4. **After-sales & spares** — 48–72 hr spares, local service, PM programme.
5. **Recognizable client references** — Adani, Jindal, Aditya Birla, L&T, defence, etc. = passed rigorous vendor qualification.
6. **Certifications** — IS 807 + IS 3177 baseline, ISO 9001, + DGMS (mining) / CE (export/MNC).
7. **Total cost of ownership** — supply + install + annual maintenance + spares score + warranty + expected structural life, not purchase price alone.

*Source: ganeshengineeringco.wordpress.com (reliability + certification + TCO sections). Confidence: High.*

## After-sales expectations
- **Warranty** — separate coverage for structural / mechanical / electrical; period stated in writing.
- **Spares** — critical parts (rope, contactors, limit switches, brake pads) stocked; 48–72 hr supply commitment.
- **Service response** — nearest service centre named; guaranteed breakdown response time; 24-hr emergency contact.
- **Preventive maintenance** — scheduled PM programme with cost (quarterly for M3–M5, monthly for M6–M8); OEM technicians.
- **Training** — operator + maintenance training per IS 13367 as part of commissioning.
- **AMC** — annual maintenance contract as the recurring-revenue relationship anchor.

*Source: ganeshengineeringco.wordpress.com (post-installation + maintenance + FAQ), revacranes.com. Confidence: High.*

## Implications for SVMH's website (Phase 2 hooks)
- Lead capture must mirror the **IS 3177 RFQ fields** → a smart RFQ form doubles as qualification and buyer education.
- Publish **industry-specific reference installs** + **factory/FAT proof** to clear the biggest trust hurdle.
- Surface **spares stocking + service response time** prominently — the maintenance-engineer gatekeeper decides on this.
- Provide a **TCO / duty-class explainer** to defeat price-only objections early.
- Keep **MD/direct + WhatsApp access** visible for the MSME persona.
