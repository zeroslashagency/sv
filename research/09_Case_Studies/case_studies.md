## Why this matters for SVMH

Competitors — from global MNCs to mid-market Indian firms — convert B2B buyers with named-customer proof (JSW, Tata, ArcelorMittal, NPCIL, etc.). Below is the competitive proof-library SVMH is up against, plus the structure that works.

## Structural takeaways for SVMH case studies
1. **Format that converts:** one page, one named customer, one problem → solution → outcome. Konecranes' single-page PDF and ElectroMech's industry-tagged pages are the two proven templates.
2. **Lead with named marquee customers by vertical.** SVMH serves automotive, steel, power, foundry, cement, construction — it needs at least one referenceable project per vertical, mirroring ElectroMech's tagging.
3. **The hot/ladle-crane niche is where the big players show off** (Anupam JSW 550T, Demag ladle brochure, Weihua 250T). SVMH claims this niche but shows no proof — even a modest ladle/foundry project write-up would differentiate it regionally.
4. **Service/AMC deserves its own case studies** (Cranedge model). SVMH's AMC revenue is invisible online; capacity-enhancement / uptime stories are low-cost, high-trust content.
5. **Repeat-order stories are the strongest trust signal** (ElectroMech–Thermax). SVMH should surface any repeat customers it has.
