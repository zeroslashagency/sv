# Competitor Brochures, Catalogues & Video Assets

> Research asset inventory for SVMH website build. Every downloadable marketing asset (PDF brochures, product catalogues, company profiles, magazines, videos) found on competitor properties.
> **Date accessed:** 2026-07-05 unless noted. **Client:** S.V. Material Handling System Pvt Ltd (SVMH).

## Why this matters for SVMH
SVMH currently has **no downloadable brochure, no catalogue, no company profile PDF, no video** on svind.co.in — it routes to IndiaMART. Every serious competitor below leads with gated or open PDF assets and a resource hub. This is the single clearest content gap to close on the new site.

## Key takeaways for the SVMH site
1. **Table-stakes gap:** even sub-scale regional rivals (Top Crane, Vikrant, S. Cranes, Shubhlaxmi) publish a downloadable PDF catalogue. SVMH has none. A single well-designed product catalogue + corporate profile PDF is the minimum viable parity move.
2. **Benchmark to beat:** ElectroMech runs a full resource hub (catalogues + vertical brochures + a 12-issue magazine + video gallery). That is the aspirational ceiling.
3. **Vertical brochures win B2B:** ElectroMech's per-industry PDFs (steel, oil & gas, infra, galvanising) map cleanly to SVMH's stated industries (automotive, steel, power, foundry, cement, construction). Recommend one-pager per vertical.
4. **Video is a differentiator:** ElectroMech, Godrej Koerber, Hercules, Konecranes all use video (factory tours, product demos). SVMH has zero multimedia — a factory/fabrication video is high-impact and low-competition regionally.
