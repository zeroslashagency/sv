# Market Gap / Hidden-Opportunity Analysis (SVMH Phase 1)

What competitors are **NOT** doing — the whitespace SVMH can own. Synthesized from the competitor profiles, 8 website audits, SEO research, and product/customer intelligence in this repo (all accessed 2026-07-05). Each gap is a candidate competitive advantage.

> **Headline:** The global/premium leaders (Konecranes, Demag, ABUS, GH, CM, Kito) have deep technical content but **weak India localization, price opacity, and friction-heavy RFQ flows**. The Indian/regional players (ElectroMech aside) have **thin technical depth, poor structured data, and little buyer education**. SVMH can win the India market by combining *premium-grade technical/product pages* with *India-first localization, transparent price guidance, on-page RFQ, and buyer education* — none of the competitors do all of this at once.

## Gap themes (how they cluster into advantages)
1. **Conversion whitespace** (gaps 1, 3, 6, 20) — competitors make buyers work to get a quote and hide price; SVMH can remove friction and be transparent.
2. **Technical-trust whitespace** (gaps 4, 5, 10, 11, 12, 17) — proof, datasheets, CAD, schema, factory evidence.
3. **Education/SEO whitespace** (gaps 7, 8, 9, 18) — India-specific buyer education and standards content nobody owns.
4. **Localization whitespace** (gaps 2, 15, 16) — India-first, dual-axis, service-forward.
5. **Aftermarket whitespace** (gaps 13, 14) — spares/service ecosystem as recurring revenue.
