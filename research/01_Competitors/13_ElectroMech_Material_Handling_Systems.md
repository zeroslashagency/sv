# Competitor Profile: ElectroMech Material Handling Systems

**Full legal name:** ElectroMech Material Handling Systems (India) Private Limited (EMHS)
**Website:** https://www.emech.com
**Date compiled:** 2026-07-05
**Compiled for:** S.V. Material Handling System Pvt Ltd (SVMH) competitive landscape research

> **One-line take:** ElectroMech is the single largest and most formidable industrial-crane competitor in India — a ~Rs 684 Cr (consolidated FY25) multinational with a 2,000-crane/year Pune plant, 10,000+ cranes installed across 60+ countries, four overseas factories, and exclusive ABUS (Germany) technology. It operates in a completely different scale tier from SVMH (~Rs 13.6 Cr).

---

## 1. Company Overview

ElectroMech is described on its own site as "counted amongst the largest manufacturers of industrial cranes and related material handling equipment in the world" and "the largest industrial overhead crane manufacturer in India and among the top ten in Asia." Founded in **1979** by Mr. Vinayak Mehendale (as "ElectroMech Engineers"), the modern entity **ElectroMech Material Handling Systems (I) Pvt Ltd was incorporated in 1996**, with manufacturing operations commencing December 2004 at Pirangut, Pune.

Since 2000 the company has been led by **Managing Director Tushar Vinayak Mehendale** (Mechanical Engineer, Government College of Engineering Pune; MS in Manufacturing Systems Engineering & Engineering Management, University of Wisconsin, USA). It has commissioned **over 10,000 cranes across more than 60 countries** and claims **60% repeat customers**.

Corporate positioning: "Cranes & Beyond" — moving beyond crane manufacturing to a full material-handling solutions "One-Stop-Shop" via strategic partnerships and its wholly owned service subsidiary **Cranedge**.

CIN: **U28160PN1996PTC099346** (per Tofler). HQ: Pune, Maharashtra, India.

## 2. Products / Solution Categories

Grouped on-site under Overhead Cranes, Hoists, Other Material Handling Equipment, and Services:

- **Overhead / EOT cranes** — single & double girder
- **Gantry & Goliath cranes** (incl. double-girder goliath)
- **Stacker cranes** (automated storage/retrieval)
- **Workshop cranes**
- **Explosion-protected (Ex) hoists & cranes** (for oil & gas / chemical)
- **Metal production cranes / process cranes / coker cranes** (steel & metals)
- **Port cranes**
- **Rubber Tyred Gantry (RTG) cranes** — launched 2022
- **Wire rope hoists & chain hoists; pneumatic/hydraulic hoists** (via J D Neuhaus)
- **Tower cranes** (via Zoomlion ElectroMech JV — construction sector)
- **Forklifts & lift trucks** (via Hyster-Yale, USA; Maximal rough-terrain)
- **Construction hoists**
- **Rollers, Pushers, Push-Pull & Manoeuvre equipment** (Easy Mover, Sweden — up to 100 t)
- **Lifting beams, HB (light crane) systems, wall-travelling & jib cranes**
- **"torqk" brand** — value range for the SME sector (launched 2012)
- **EMote** — IoT/industrial-internet monitoring for EOT cranes
- **Services** — erection & installation, modification, AMC, repairs, equipment leasing, spares (via Cranedge)

**Overlap with SVMH:** EOT (single/double girder), gantry/goliath, jib, workshop cranes, hoists, crane components, and AMC/service all directly overlap SVMH's core catalog.

## 4. Manufacturing Capabilities & Factory Locations

- **Main plant: Pirangut, Pune, India** — annual capacity **2,000 standard cranes/year**; described as "amongst the largest dedicated overhead crane manufacturing facilities in the world." Site spread cited as **120,000 sq.m** (homepage) / factory built-up ~20,000 sq.m at Pirangut on 72,000 sq.m land (historical timeline).
- Equipment: CNC cutting machines, semi-automated girder-manufacturing fixtures, shot-blasting systems, one of the industry's largest paint booths, dedicated load-testing facilities, SAP ERP backbone.
- **Overseas manufacturing plants: India, Dubai (UAE), Indonesia, and Saudi Arabia** (per homepage meta and About). Saudi factory = "ElectroMech Industry Company" (2022); Indonesia = "PT. ElectroMech Manufacturing Industries."
- Engineering team of **40+ design engineers**; ~250 service engineers (via Cranedge) across India (per CARE Ratings).

**Domestic sales & service offices:** Ahmedabad, Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai (CARE Ratings). *(Note: ElectroMech has a Bangalore presence — SVMH's home city.)*

## 5. Export Markets

Exports began **2001** (first orders from UAE and Oman). Now references and crane supply across **60+ countries**. Overseas subsidiaries handle Middle East, CIS, Africa (ElectroMech FZE, Jebel Ali Dubai — 2009), Abu Dhabi Oil & Gas (Equipment Trading LLC — 2015), and South-East Asia (Indonesia — 2018). Won multiple Engineering Export Promotion Council (EEPC) Export Excellence Awards (2005, 2013, 2015). Note: per CARE Ratings, **~80%+ of revenue is domestic** — export is meaningful but the company is primarily India-driven.

## Threat to SVMH

**Severity: Very High (as a benchmark) / Moderate (as a direct head-to-head threat).**

- **Scale gap is enormous:** ElectroMech's consolidated FY25 revenue (~Rs 684 Cr) is roughly **50x** SVMH's ~Rs 13.6 Cr. EMHS is a debt-free, credit-rated (CARE A) multinational; SVMH is a family MSME. Direct competition on large turnkey/nuclear/refinery projects is not realistic for SVMH today.
- **Geographic overlap:** ElectroMech operates a Bangalore sales & service office — directly in SVMH's home market — and its Cranedge arm aggressively pursues AMC/service on "any make, anywhere," which threatens SVMH's spares + AMC revenue stream (a core SVMH line).
- **Product overlap:** Full overlap on EOT single/double girder, gantry/goliath, jib, hoists, and components. EMHS's ABUS-backed "standardized" cranes and its SME-focused **"torqk"** brand let it reach down-market toward SVMH's price tier.
- **Digital benchmark:** EMHS is the gold standard SVMH's new website should study — strong SEO, deep blog, industry-segmented navigation, clear solution taxonomy, video content, and a services-led narrative (Cranedge). SVMH's IndiaMART-dependent presence is far behind. **Actionable lesson for SVMH's site:** industry-vertical landing pages, standards/certification proof, downloadable catalogs, case studies with named clients, and a distinct service/AMC section.
- **Where SVMH can compete:** localized responsiveness, price on lower-tonnage cranes, foundry/ladle/hot-metal niche cranes, and faster turnaround for regional/MSME buyers who find ElectroMech's project machinery over-specced or over-priced.
