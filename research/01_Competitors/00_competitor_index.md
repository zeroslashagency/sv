# Industrial Crane Competitor Index — SVMH Phase 1

This index consolidates the competitor landscape for SVMH's Phase 1 research across the industrial/overhead crane market. Competitors are grouped by tier, from global multinational leaders down to same-city Bengaluru rivals and adjacent-industry players. Each row notes the company's headquarters, website, and why it is relevant as a benchmark or direct competitor to SVMH's crane, hoist, spares, fabrication, and AMC/service lines. Confidence levels and sources (accessed 2026-07-05) are captured in the "Why relevant" column. Some organizations appear more than once because the source data captured them from multiple listings or under both their global and India-specific entities; these are preserved as-is to reflect the raw research pass.
