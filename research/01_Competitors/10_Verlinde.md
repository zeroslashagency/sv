# Competitor Profile: Verlinde (VERLINDE SAS)

**Research date:** 2026-07-05
**Analyst context:** Prepared for S.V. Material Handling System Pvt Ltd (SVMH), Bengaluru, to inform competitive-landscape and website strategy.
**Primary domain:** https://www.verlinde.com

---

## 1. Company Overview

Verlinde (legal entity **VERLINDE SAS**) is a French manufacturer and exporter of lifting and material-handling equipment, headquartered in Vernouillet (Eure-et-Loir), France. It is one of the oldest continuously operating hoist makers in the world, founded in **Lille in 1858** by Léon Verlinde as a hardware workshop ("La Truelle d'Or") producing pulleys, couplings and shafts for the region's textile mills. In 1872 Auguste Verlinde designed and patented a worm-drive hoist, establishing the company's material-handling engineering focus.

Since **1993 Verlinde has been part of the Finnish Konecranes Group** (one of the world's largest crane and lifting-equipment groups). Verlinde operates as a distinct brand within Konecranes, positioned as "the leading French manufacturer and exporter of lifting and handling equipment." It serves both **industrial** and **entertainment ("show-business")** markets, the latter through its well-known STAGEMAKER brand of theatrical/rigging chain hoists.

The company markets a comprehensive range described as **~30 groups of lifting equipment covering capacities from roughly 60 kg to 250,000 kg (250 t)**. Distribution is channel-led: in France through a sales/after-sales network plus the **EUROPONT** franchise network of travelling-crane construction plants (the first national network of overhead-crane manufacturers, set up 1993); abroad through consultants/distributors in **over 92 countries**.

Recent leadership change: **Pierre Eluard** was appointed Managing Director in **March 2024**, succeeding **François Gurniki** (who led for ~7 years). Under Eluard the company has pursued international expansion, opening a **Singapore office (Oct 2024)** and a **Dubai office (Nov 2024)** covering the Middle East & Africa region.

## 4. Product Categories

Verlinde's catalogue spans the full lifting-equipment stack:

**Electric hoists & winches**
- **EUROBLOC VT / VF / VM** — electric wire rope hoists, loads ~800 to 80,000 kg (VT design holds 13 patents)
- **EUROCHAIN VX / VL** — electric chain hoists, loads ~63 to 10,000 kg
- **PDW** — process-duty open winches, loads ~6,300 to 70,000 kg (duty group up to M6/M7), for heavy/continuous applications (e.g. biomass power stations)
- **TIRLIFT / TIRLIFT 2** — electric powered wire-rope pulling winches
- **STAGEMAKER SL / SR** — electric chain hoists for entertainment/show-business rigging, loads ~125 to 5,000 kg (double-brake, CHAINFlux chain guide, Perfect Push load wheel)

**Manual hoists & handling**
- Manual chain hoists, hand chain blocks (ZHR heavy-duty), lever hoists (PLV), manual/electric travel trolleys, ME winches

**Cranes & structures**
- **EUROSYSTEM STD / ALD** — overhead handling systems (steel & aluminium hollow-profile)
- **EUROSTYLE** — jib cranes and travelling cranes
- Jib cranes (wall/column-mounted, articulated — VFM motorized jib)
- **VGIM / VGPA / VGPS** — gantry and portal cranes (mobile workshop gantries, loads 500–2,000 kg steel)
- Overhead travelling cranes (single-girder, double-girder, box girder) built via EUROPONT network partners, up to ~40+ tonnes
- Heavy travelling-crane components (crab units, end carriages, etc.)

**Electronic products**
- **EUROMOTE / DIGIMOTE** — radio remote-control systems for hoists and cranes
- **ExpertCare** — smartphone app for remote monitoring of EUROBLOC VT hoists (condition/usage data from floor level)
- Variators / control electronics

**Specialised / custom machines**
- Flame-retarding (spark-proof) equipment; customized machines for entertainment, rail, nuclear, aerospace and automotive industries; equipment for explosive (ATEX) environments.

## 6. Manufacturing Capabilities

- Mass production of **wire rope, chain and belt electric winches**, plus travelling-crane components, at its own production plants.
- Vernouillet (Eure-et-Loir) is described as **one of the Konecranes group's major industrial plants**; the site was heavily reshaped (counter to relocation trends) and production capacity was set to double by 2010.
- A 2008-era Scribd company profile cites a facility of ~9,600 m² producing wire rope hoists, chain hoists and crane components.
- R&D-intensive: 50+ patents historically; innovations include the disk brake, hydraulic/articulated bridge, reverse hoists (1975, entertainment), double brake, and short large-diameter drums (EUROBLOC VT).
- Component materials and parts subjected to "most stringent checks" under ISO-certified QMS.

## 17. Threat to SVMH

**Relevance: Medium-High as a benchmark; Low-Medium as a direct India competitor.**

- **Direct product overlap:** Verlinde makes exactly what SVMH makes — EOT/overhead travelling cranes (single/double girder), gantry/portal, jib cranes, monorail systems, wire-rope and chain hoists, crane components (crab units, trolleys, drums, sheaves). Both target automotive, steel, foundry, power and construction sectors.
- **Scale & backing:** Verlinde is far larger in reach (Konecranes-owned, 92+ countries, 168-year heritage, ISO 9001/14001, 50+ patents). It competes at the premium/branded end where SVMH competes on price/local service.
- **India entry risk:** Verlinde already appears on India industry portals and sells globally through distributors; the Konecranes group has a strong India presence. If Verlinde pushes its distributor/EUROPONT model into India, it could pressure SVMH on higher-value, standards-driven (CE/FEM) projects — especially with export-oriented or MNC customers in India who value a European brand.
- **Website lesson for SVMH:** Verlinde is a model for what SVMH's site should aspire to — strong multilingual product catalogue, downloadable brochures/certificates, a steady case-study/news feed, distributor locator, and IoT/"4.0" product storytelling (ExpertCare). SVMH's reliance on IndiaMART/ExportersIndia is a clear gap versus this standard.
- **SVMH's defensible edge:** Local Bengaluru manufacturing, fabrication + AMC service proximity, IS 807/IS 3177/FEM 9.511 compliance for the Indian market, and price competitiveness — areas where a France-based premium exporter is structurally disadvantaged.
