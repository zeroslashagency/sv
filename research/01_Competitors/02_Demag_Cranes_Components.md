## 1. Company Overview

Demag Cranes & Components GmbH is one of the world's leading manufacturers of light crane systems, industrial/process cranes, hoists (rope and chain), crane components, motors and drives. The brand traces its roots to 1819 (Mechanische Werkstätten Harkort & Co., Wetter an der Ruhr) and full crane production from 1840; the "Demag" name (Deutsche Maschinenfabrik AG) dates to 1910. After a long ownership chain (Mannesmann → Siemens/Bosch → KKR → Terex), the industrial crane business was acquired by Finland's **Konecranes** in 2017 and today operates as a wholly owned Konecranes subsidiary. Demag markets itself as having served customers "for more than 200 years."

- **Legal entity:** Demag Cranes & Components GmbH
- **Registered seat:** Düsseldorf, Germany (Forststraße 16, 40597 Düsseldorf; HRB 72305, Amtsgericht Düsseldorf) — per 2024 policy statement.
- **Main operating/visitor site & historic HQ:** Ruhrstraße 28, 58300 Wetter (Ruhr), Germany.
- **Parent:** Konecranes Plc (Hyvinkää, Finland; listed on Nasdaq Helsinki; ~16,500 employees group-wide).
- **Core products (per official self-description):** "light crane systems, crane components, motors, drives and spare parts."

## 3. Product Categories

Verified from the official products page (https://www.demagcranes.com/en-us/products):

- **KBK light/modular crane systems:** KBK Aluline (aluminium), stacker cranes, overhung & extending cranes, single- and double-girder suspension cranes, suspension monorails, freestanding/workstation cranes, pillar & wall-mounted slewing jibs.
- **Cranes & crane sets:** Process cranes (incl. process cranes for steel production & foundries), universal cranes, V-type crane (patented lightweight), crane kits, crane sets for overhead/underrunning/top-running cranes, wall-mounted jib crane sets, Free Standing Steel Structure (FSS).
- **Hoist units:** Rope hoists (DH, DHR, DR-Pro, DVR, DMR, DS10-Wind winch), chain hoists (DC-Pro, DC-Com, DC-Wind, DCS-Pro, LDC-Q quadro, double chain hoists, Manulift), balancers (DCBS electric balancer), belt hoist (DBH, new 2026).
- **Drives:** Crane end carriages (DFO/DFM, KTL), gear motors (inline, offset, right-angle, FG microspeed), motors (conical-rotor & cylindrical-rotor brake motors), wheel range (RS/DRS wheel blocks, DWS/LRS wheel sets), frequency inverters (Dedrive Compact STO, Dedrive Pro), transfer carriages.
- **Components:** Power supply lines / conductor lines (DCL-Pro), control pendants (DSE/DSK/DST/DSC), radio controls (Demag D3, DRC family), HCW high-capacity winch crabs, open winch trolleys.
- **Digital/service:** Demag StatusControl 2.0 (remote condition monitoring), D.A.S.S. safety software, apps.

*Note:* Standard cranes are typically quoted up to ~50 t and process cranes up to ~300 t in Demag literature (Medium confidence — third-party product-range summary).

## 4. Industries Served

Dedicated industry pages confirm: **Automotive** (OEM assembly, body-in-white, in-house logistics), **Power/Energy** (gas & steam turbine manufacturing), **Steel production & Foundries** (process/ladle cranes), **Wind energy** (DC-Wind chain hoist, DS10-Wind winch for on/offshore), **Paper**, **Industrial manufacturing / machine shops**, **Logistics & warehousing**, **Construction/precast elements**, **Shipbuilding/ports** (historically, and via Konecranes/Gottwald). Case studies also show camper/motorhome, insulation, and consumer-goods manufacturers.

## 5. Manufacturing Capabilities & Factory Locations

- **Germany — Wetter (Ruhr):** Main plant; rope hoists, chain hoists (DC-II built here), cranes, spare-parts distribution centre (5,000+ pallet spaces, ~13,000 spare parts shippable within 24h). Received "Best Factory 2010" Industrial Excellence Award.
- **Germany — Uslar:** Engine/motor plant (conical-rotor brake motors), operating 50+ years (2022 anniversary).
- **Czech Republic — Slaný:** Process/heavy crane manufacturing (established under KKR ~2003).
- The 2024 human-rights policy statement confirms Demag Cranes & Components GmbH's "two manufacturing sites in Germany, in Wetter and in Uslar," plus purchasing from other Konecranes companies (i.e. cranes are also built through the wider Konecranes/global network).

## 6. Export Markets & Global Presence

Demag states a "global presence with a large network of partners and distributors" and runs an **Official Country Partner** distributor program. Historic export intensity was very high (>80% of sales at times pre-WWII). Country sites include US, India, Middle East (FZE), plus Southeast Asia operations (formerly MHE-Demag, acquired by Konecranes 2020, factories historically in Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Philippines). An unofficial reseller cites "85 countries" and "6,500+ installations" (Low confidence — not an official Demag figure).

## Threat to SVMH

Demag is a **premium global incumbent directly present in SVMH's backyard** (Pune, via Konecranes and Demag Pvt Ltd) with 200+ years of brand equity, standardized/certified products (ISO 9001/14001/45001), and a heavily digital go-to-market (online shop with 16,000+ SKUs, CAD configurators, remote monitoring). It competes head-on with SVMH's EOT/process/foundry/ladle cranes and components (hoists, drives, DSL/conductor lines, end carriages) — and it targets the same industries (automotive, steel, power, foundry).

Demag does **not** compete on price or local-SME agility: it plays the high-spec, high-trust, engineered-solution tier. **Implications for the SVMH website:** SVMH should not try to out-scale Demag but should out-localize — emphasize IS 807 / IS 3177 / FEM 9.511 compliance, Bengaluru/South-India proximity, faster AMC/spares turnaround, and cost-competitive custom fabrication. The gap SVMH must close digitally is credibility signaling (certifications badges, case studies with named clients, downloadable spec sheets, load-capacity/duty-class clarity) — all areas where Demag sets the visible benchmark.
