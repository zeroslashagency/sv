## Company Overview

Street Crane is positioned as **"the UK's largest overhead crane and hoist manufacturer"** and the country's only volume wire-rope hoist maker. Founded in 1946 by Peter Street (a WWII Spitfire pilot) with an £88 RAF gratuity, repairing neglected workshop cranes in Sheffield, the firm grew into one of the world's leading manufacturers of overhead cranes and hoists. It remains family-owned across three-to-four generations:

- **Peter Street** — founder
- **Martin Street** — Chairman (celebrating 50 years with the company in 2025; born ~1954, director since 1998)
- **William (Peter) Street** — Finance Director (4th generation)
- **Gus (Augusto) Zona** — Group CEO / Managing Director (Italian national; joined 1988 as a Production Clerk, rose internally)
- **Mark Baslington** — Technical Director (Designate), appointed 2026
- **Anthony Robert McClure** — Operations Director (since 2019)

Business model is described internally as an **"alpha-beta" strategy**: (a) building complete cranes for end users in the UK, and (b) selling crane **components/kits** to resellers and a worldwide dealer network. Street developed its own hoist range ~60 years ago rather than depend on third-party hoist merchants — now in its 6th generation of hoist (ZX). After-sales/service is run through sister company **Street Crane Express** (spares & repairs).

> "Street Crane is the last original engineering manufacturer in the UK and is still a family-owned business." — streetcrane.com/about

**Self-reported scale stats (homepage):** 80 years experience · 30,400+ projects completed · 70 countries.

## Product Categories

**Cranes** (pre-engineered, up to 250 t, spans to 45 m):
- Overhead cranes — single & double girder
- Underslung cranes
- Portal / Goliath cranes
- Jib cranes
- Light / workstation cranes
- Specialised ("special") cranes — bespoke engineered

**Hoists & Components** (capacities 125 kg – 250 t):
- **ZX** electric wire rope hoists (flagship, 6th generation; models incl. ZX8, ZX10)
- **VX** open winch hoists
- **LX** electric chain hoists
- Specialised / bespoke hoists
- Crane kits (everything to build a crane except the main bridge girder steelwork)
- End carriages / end trucks

**Spares & Servicing:** genuine Street parts via UK and USA warehouses and global distributors; installation, maintenance, condition monitoring. Smart controls, anti-sway technology, condition monitoring promoted as innovation.

## Manufacturing Capabilities & Factory Locations

- **HQ & main factory:** Town End Works, Chapel-en-le-Frith, High Peak, Derbyshire (Peak District), UK — described as "some of the most up-to-date manufacturing facilities in the world."
- Opened a **£3M dedicated hoist factory** in 2015.
- In-house capability across fabrication, machining, and electrical engineering; heavy investment in **apprenticeships** (no dedicated crane-industry training exists, so they grow talent internally).
- **USA operation:** Street Crane Inc. — established a Charleston, South Carolina facility, **relocating in 2026 to a 30,000 sq ft facility in Greenville, SC** (also listed at Duncan, SC / Duncan Reidville Rd). 11 US staff; holds finished stock for next-day US delivery. US sales reported ~80% up on forecast in 2026.
- Stated expansion roadmap: **US first, then Europe, then Middle East.**

## Threat to SVMH

**High relevance / medium-to-high threat.** Street Crane is actively and publicly expanding in **India** — SVMH's home market — through distributor partners (Skyline Industries, Visdal Ventures, Cranesmith) delivering the exact products SVMH makes: single/double-girder EOT cranes, and mould/die-handling cranes for automotive and manufacturing. Their April 2026 India case-study campaign signals deliberate market push.

Where Street out-competes SVMH:
- **Brand & scale:** 80-year heritage, £37.7M turnover (~≥17× SVMH's ~₹13.6 Cr / ~£1.3M), blue-chip client logos (JCB, BMW, Tata, BAE, Rolls-Royce).
- **Digital presence (the core of this benchmarking):** modern WordPress site, online **crane configurator with pricing**, **serial-number spares portal**, rich case studies, active LinkedIn (12k followers), brochures, video. This is precisely the gap where SVMH is weak (reliant on IndiaMART/ExportersIndia).
- **Own hoist IP** (ZX/VX/LX ranges) vs SVMH's component-assembly model.
- **Certifications:** ISO 9001 + ISO 45001 vs SVMH's ISO 9001 only.

Where SVMH can counter / defend:
- Street sells into India **via distributors** (imported UK kits) — higher landed cost, longer lead times (UK build 8–16 weeks + shipping) and tariff/logistics exposure. SVMH is a **local manufacturer** with price, lead-time, AMC/service-proximity, and IS-code (IS 807/3177/FEM 9.511) compliance advantages.
- SVMH offers full fabrication + on-site AMC service locally; Street relies on third-party distributor service in India.
- **Actionable takeaway for the website project:** SVMH should mirror Street's digital strengths — structured product taxonomy (crane types + components), industry landing pages, real case studies with capacities/spans, a spec/enquiry configurator, downloadable brochures, and prominent certifications — while leaning hard into "local manufacturer, faster, IS-compliant, lifetime AMC" positioning against an importer.
