## 1. Company Overview

STAHL CraneSystems GmbH is a German manufacturer of hoists and crane components, headquartered in Künzelsau, Baden-Württemberg. It is one of the world's leading builders of hoisting technology and crane components and is the recognized global market leader for **explosion-protected (Ex / flame-proof) lifting technology**. Founded in 1876 in Stuttgart by Rafael Stahl and Gustav Weineck, the company has more than a century of crane-building heritage.

A critical strategic distinction: since 2009 STAHL CraneSystems **withdrew from building complete cranes** and repositioned itself as a supplier of hoists, crane components and modular "CraneKits" sold to a certified network of "Authorised Crane Building Partners" who design, build, install and service the finished cranes. This is a fundamentally different business model from SVMH (which builds and services complete cranes directly).

Since **2017, STAHL CraneSystems is a brand of Columbus McKinnon Corporation (CMCO)**, a NASDAQ-listed US intelligent-motion group. CMCO acquired STAHL for approximately **USD 240 million**. The STAHL web presence now lives entirely under the cmco.com domain.

## 3. Product Categories

STAHL markets one of the widest hoist and crane-component ranges in the world. Core categories:

**Electric wire rope hoists**
- SXD / SXF Wire Rope Hoist (latest generation, up to 10,000 kg)
- SH Wire Rope Hoist
- AS 7 Wire Rope Hoist
- TDC Twin Drive Concept
- SH Ex / AS 7 Ex (explosion-protected variants)
- LNG Wire Rope Hoists (special hoists for LNG tanks)

**Electric chain hoists**
- ST Chain Hoist
- STK Chain Hoist
- STF Chain Hoist
- STD Dual Chain Hoist
- STB Chain Hoist (Big Bag)
- ST Ex Chain Hoist (explosion-protected)

**Winches**
- SHW 8 Winch (electric winch; 125 kg up to ~5 tons per marketing)

**Crane components**
- Endcarriages / end trucks for suspension and overhead travelling (O.H.T.) cranes
- Travel drives
- Crane electrics (standard and explosion-proof)
- Wheel blocks

**CraneKits (modular crane-building systems)**
- CraneKits
- CraneKit for monorail systems and jib cranes

**Crane systems (via partners, not built by STAHL directly):** slewing jib cranes, single- and double-girder overhead travelling cranes, suspension cranes, Goliath/gantry and semi-portal cranes, bracket cranes. Stated capacity range: **125 kg to 250,000 kg (250 t)**.

**Overlap with SVMH:** Strong overlap in hoists, wire rope hoists, crane components (end carriages, wheel blocks, travel drives), single/double girder cranes, gantry, jib and monorail. STAHL's unique edge is explosion-protected/LNG lifting, which SVMH does not appear to offer.

## 5. Manufacturing Capabilities & Factory Locations

- **Primary manufacturing:** Künzelsau, Germany (Hofratsmühle plant, relocated there from Stuttgart 1944/1963). "State of the art manufacturing facility in Künzelsau ... large capacity for manufacturing all types of hoist, travel drives, end carriages, wheel and controls" (STAHL India, IndiaMART).
- **Explosion Protection heritage sites:** Waldenburg (Ex division relocated 2001).
- Manufacturing focus is on hoists and standardized crane-component kits ("Made in Germany" positioning) shipped to crane-building partners globally who perform fabrication, assembly, erection and commissioning locally.
- STAHL emphasizes engineering / customized ("off-standard") hoist production — self-described world market leader for off-standard hoisting equipment.

Confidence: High for Künzelsau as manufacturing HQ; Medium on the precise current plant footprint under CMCO ownership.

## 6. India Presence (highly SVMH-relevant)

STAHL operates **Stahl Cranesystems (India) Private Limited**, headquartered in **Chennai** (CIN prefix U29150TN…PTC…27; GST state code 33 = Tamil Nadu; GST registered Aug 2017; banker ICICI; ~18 years listed on IndiaMART).

- **Model in India:** STAHL India acts as principal/importer supplying German-made "crane kits" (hoists + components) to a network of **Authorised Crane Building Partners** who build, supply and service complete cranes. STAHL does not itself fabricate complete cranes in India.
- **Partner / office locations:** Chennai (HQ), Bangalore, Coimbatore, Pune, Hyderabad, Faridabad/Delhi, Kolkata.
- **India scale claims:** "200 satisfied customers & 500 installations in India" (benefits page); LinkedIn (STAHL CraneSystems India) claims "more than 350+ customers in India" and positions as market leader in explosion-protected (flame-proof) hoists & crane components.
- **Annual turnover (India entity):** Rs 5–25 Cr band (IndiaMART factsheet).
- **Notable India reference:** Sparkline (STAHL's Indian crane-building partner) built fully automated process cranes for a **Ford plant in India** (stahlcranes.com press).
- Middle East arm: **STAHL CraneSystems FZE (UAE)** serves the Middle East.

**Direct competitive note:** STAHL competes in SVMH's home city (Bangalore is a listed partner location) and sells into the same automotive/steel/power segments — but at a premium "German technology at Indian cost" positioning, and specifically owns the explosion-proof niche.

## 8. Certifications

- **ISO 9001:2015** quality management certification.
- **ATEX** – products comply with EU product directive 2014/34/EU (ATEX 95); operation guidance per ATEX 1999/92/EC (ATEX 137).
- **IECEx** – all STAHL products available with IECEx certification (international scheme for equipment in explosive atmospheres).
- **CE** conformity marking (EU) as part of ATEX conformity assessment.

STAHL's certification depth in explosion protection (ATEX + IECEx across the full product line) is a genuine technical differentiator vs a general Indian crane maker like SVMH (which holds ISO 9001, plus GST/MSME, and builds to IS 807 / IS 3177 / FEM 9.511).

Confidence: High (ISO 9001:2015, ATEX, IECEx stated explicitly on cmco.com legal-principles page).

## 11. Years in Business

- Founded **1876** — ~150 years of history (2026).
- "More than 100 years of experience in crane building" (STAHL India).
- STAHL CraneSystems brand as currently constituted created **2005** (spun out of R. STAHL when the conveyor-technology division was sold; R. STAHL sold that division to Konecranes in 2005 and the STAHL CraneSystems premium brand was created).
- Part of Columbus McKinnon since **2017**.

Key history milestones (from cmco.com company history):
- 1876 founded (Stuttgart); 1898 first large electric overhead crane; **1926 first explosion-proof electrical devices** (origin of Ex leadership); 1944 production moved to Künzelsau; 1979 first US subsidiary; 2005 STAHL CraneSystems brand created; 2009 exited full crane construction + launched certified partner network; 2017 acquired by Columbus McKinnon.

## Threat to SVMH

**Overall threat level: Medium (niche-specific: High).**

- **Different model, overlapping turf.** STAHL does not build complete cranes in India — it sells premium German hoists/kits through crane-building partners. It is present in **Bangalore** (SVMH's home city) and targets the same automotive/steel/power/cement/foundry/construction verticals. In practice STAHL competes on *components and premium hoists*, and its partners compete for the *finished-crane* orders SVMH chases.
- **Explosion-proof moat.** STAHL owns the ATEX/IECEx flame-proof lifting niche (oil & gas, chemical, LNG, pharma). SVMH has no visible offering here — any SVMH prospect with hazardous-area requirements will default to STAHL. This is a segment SVMH cannot easily contest.
- **Brand & digital gulf.** STAHL has 150 years of heritage, a Columbus McKinnon parent, multilingual sites, a document library, partner portal, active LinkedIn/Instagram/YouTube, and published case studies. SVMH's IndiaMART-dependent presence looks small next to this. For the website rebuild, STAHL is a good *aspirational* benchmark for: structured product taxonomy, case studies, downloadable brochures/specs, and industry-segment landing pages.
- **Where SVMH can win.** STAHL's Indian pricing is premium ("German technology at Indian cost") and it relies on partners for build/service. SVMH can position on: direct manufacturer-to-customer relationship, in-house fabrication + AMC/service, faster/local turnaround, price competitiveness for standard IS 807/IS 3177/FEM cranes, and full-crane accountability (single vendor vs partner chain). The website should emphasize these as explicit differentiators.
