## Executive Summary

Anupam Industries Limited (brand: Anupam Group / Anupam Cranes) is one of India's largest and oldest overhead crane manufacturers, headquartered in Vithal Udyognagar (near Anand), Gujarat. Founded in 1973 by Mr. J.C. Patel, the company has 50+ years of experience, 5,000+ crane installations, a manufacturing capacity of ~45,000 TPA, and a global export footprint (USA, UAE, Brazil, Azerbaijan, Turkey). It positions itself as "India's largest supplier of overhead cranes" and is uniquely credited as the only Indian company to manufacture, erect, and commission 550T-capacity hot metal ladle handling cranes.

This is a **far larger, heavier, more globally credentialed player than SVMH** — operating at the high-capacity steel-mill / port / infrastructure end of the market (up to 550T), versus SVMH's SME standard-crane positioning. See "Threat to SVMH" at the bottom.

## Company Overview

Established in 1973 by Mr. J.C. Patel, Anupam Industries is described as "one of India's leading overhead crane manufacturers & suppliers." The current CMD is Mehul Jagdishchandra Patel. Over 50+ years it has "supplied and commissioned over 5,000 cranes up to 550T capacity" to Power, Steel, Construction, Cement, Shipyard, Fertilizers & Petrochemicals, and Heavy OEM industries.

The company markets itself (on LinkedIn) as "India's largest supplier of overhead cranes, with a manufacturing capacity exceeding 45,000 TPA."

> "With our facility stretching across 1,00,000 square meters in and around Anand, we have been manufacturing a wide range of cranes for the last 50+ years, having supplied and commissioned over 5,000 cranes up to 550T capacity..." — About page (accessed 2026-07-05)

**Notable corporate development:** In 2010–11 Anupam signed JV terms with Mitsubishi Heavy Industries (Japan) and received private equity investment from Aditya Birla Private Equity — signals of scale and outside capital well beyond a typical SME crane maker. (Note: Tracxn also lists 10 historical loans/charges including large Edelweiss ARC facilities, suggesting a past debt-restructuring history — Confidence: Medium, banded data.)

## Product Categories

Seven headline product lines (each with a dedicated page):

1. **Electric Overhead Travel (EOT) Cranes** — single/double/4-girder; up to 550T. Sub-range: steel mill cranes (scrap yard, semi-finished/process, finished product, service & maintenance), OEM/heavy-duty cranes.
2. **Goliath Cranes** (gantry)
3. **Charging & Teeming Cranes** (steelmaking)
4. **Container Handling Cranes** (ship-to-shore / yard)
5. **Bulk Material Handling Equipment** — ship unloaders (SUL), grab ship unloaders
6. **Wharf / Jib Cranes** (port loading/unloading)
7. **Tower Cranes** (CE-engineered, dual slewing; tech from ALFA SRL Italy)

Specialised: Hot metal ladle handling cranes up to 550T; Rail Mounted Quay (RMQ) cranes; Rubber Tyred Gantry (RTG) & Rail Mounted Gantry (RMG) cranes; single-failure-proof cranes (nuclear/BARC); forging cranes.

## Manufacturing Capabilities & Facilities

- **Footprint:** ~100,000 sq m total (50,000 covered + 50,000 open) in/around Anand, Gujarat.
- **Capacity:** 40,000–45,000 TPA; output 300–400 cranes/year.
- **Units (per Infrastructure page):**
  - **Unit-1:** Corporate & admin hub (Marketing, HR, IT, Commercial) — ~190 staff.
  - **Unit-2:** Fabrication & assembly (medium-large cranes); up to 25 cranes/yr; load testing to 350T.
  - **Unit-4:** Largest complex — 75,000 sq m, 5 bays incl. 10,000 sq m painting + 10,000 sq m CNC machining + dedicated tower-crane bay; up to 250 cranes/yr; load testing to 250T.
  - **Unit-5:** Integrated engineering & production — 15,000 sq m; heavy bay (75T); up to 50 cranes/yr; load testing to 500T.
  - (History: 6 units built between 2006–2012; site now references 4 manufacturing units.)
- **Capabilities:** CNC plasma cutting, heavy structural fabrication, wire-cut CNC, boring machines, heavy lathes, plate rolling, shot blasting, NDT, third-party/customer-witnessed testing.
- **Design/Engineering:** In-house — Solid Edge & 3D modeling, FEA via ANSYS & STAAD Pro, dedicated R&D team.

## Threat to SVMH

**Tier of competitor: High-end / heavyweight — different weight class than SVMH.**

- **Scale gap:** Anupam runs ~100,000 sq m across multiple units, 300–400 cranes/yr, ~₹100–500 Cr revenue, 400–600 staff, and 5,000+ installs — versus SVMH's ~₹13.6 Cr, family-owned SME footprint. Anupam is roughly an order of magnitude larger.
- **Capability gap:** Anupam credibly delivers up to 550T steel-mill/ladle/gantry cranes with ASME/CMAA/FEM/DIN pedigree and global exports (USA, UAE, Brazil). SVMH's IS 807/IS 3177/FEM 9.511 standard-duty EOT/gantry/jib range does **not** overlap with Anupam's flagship heavy steel-mill work.
- **Where they overlap:** Standard EOT/gantry/jib cranes, spares/components, and AMC — Anupam's lower-capacity EOT and OEM cranes compete directly with SVMH's core, and Anupam's brand + certifications give it a strong pull for automotive/power/cement buyers evaluating both.
- **Digital lesson for SVMH's new website:** Anupam demonstrates the target standard — sector-specific industry pages, quantified capability stats, named case studies with client testimonials, client-logo walls, certification badges, and clear CTAs. SVMH (currently reliant on IndiaMART/ExportersIndia) is heavily out-marketed online. Anupam's own gap (placeholder "lorem ipsum" news, catalog download links that don't resolve) is a minor opportunity SVMH can beat with working, downloadable spec sheets and genuine SEO content.
- **Bottom line:** Anupam is unlikely to chase SVMH's small-ticket SME orders directly, but it sets the aspirational benchmark and will win any buyer who cross-shops on scale, certifications, or export credibility. SVMH should compete on responsiveness, price, local: regional service, and niche customization rather than head-to-head on heavy capacity.
