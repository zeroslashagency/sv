## 1. Company Overview

Konecranes Oyj (Plc) is a Finnish, publicly listed (Nasdaq Helsinki: KCR) global leader in lifting and material handling. It designs, manufactures and services overhead/industrial cranes, wire rope & chain hoists, workstation lifting systems, lift trucks, and port/container-handling equipment, and runs the world's largest crane service network. The group tagline is "Moves what matters."

- **Headquarters:** Hyvinkää, Finland.
- **Founded:** April 15, 1994 (spun out of KONE Corporation's crane division; crane heritage traces to 1933 when KONE began building Electric Overhead Traveling Cranes). This makes it a ~32-year-old standalone company with ~90+ years of crane lineage — vs. SVMH's founding in 2006.
- **Legal/stock:** Julkinen osakeyhtiö (public limited); ISIN FI0009005870; listed on Helsinki exchange since 1996.
- **CEO:** Marko Tulokas (President & CEO, appointed May 15, 2025). Chairman: Pasi Laine. CFO: Teo Ottola.
- **Scale claim:** "Produces about one in ten of the world's cranes," ~80% for factories and the rest for ports (per Wikipedia citing FT/Bloomberg). One of the largest crane manufacturers in the world.
- **Growth model:** Heavy acquisition track record — ~100 companies acquired over ~40 years, including the transformative 2017 acquisition of Terex MHPS (which brought back the Demag brand).

## 3. Product Categories

**Industrial Equipment (Business Area):**
- Overhead cranes (single & double girder) — light and heavy industrial lifting up to ~500 tons; brands/series include CXT (wire rope hoist crane, "industry benchmark in medium-heavy lifting"), S-series (synthetic rope hoist), Smart CXT, M-series.
- Wire rope hoists and chain hoists (Konecranes is "the world's largest supplier of industrial cranes and wire rope hoists").
- Workstation lifting systems (KBK modular rail systems, light lifting).
- Customized load-handling attachments; process cranes for demanding duty.
- Lift trucks & reach stackers (via KC Lift Trucks / SMV heritage).
- Agilon — automated, patented in-house material-handling/warehouse system.
- Smart features: Sway Control, Shock Load Prevention, Protected Areas, remote monitoring.

**Port Solutions (Business Area):** ship-to-shore (STS) container cranes, RTG/RMG, straddle carriers, automated stacking cranes, reach stackers, terminal automation software.

**Industrial Service (Business Area):** maintenance, inspections (CARE preventive maintenance program), modernization/retrofits, spare parts for all makes & models, TRUCONNECT remote services.

*Overlap with SVMH:* EOT/overhead cranes, wire rope hoists, crane components/spares, and AMC-style service programs directly overlap SVMH's core catalogue — but Konecranes competes on brand, automation, and a global service network SVMH cannot match.

## 4. Industries Served

General manufacturing, automotive, steel/metal production, paper & pulp, power generation, raw materials, chemical, shipyards, ports/container terminals, waste-to-energy, mining, nuclear, cement. (SVMH's target industries — automotive, steel, power, foundry, cement, construction — sit entirely inside Konecranes' served set.)

## 5. Manufacturing Capabilities & Factory Locations

- **Global manufacturing:** "Our products are manufactured on all continents in our own factories, as well as through global subcontractor networks, and sold worldwide."
- Historic/known plants: Hyvinkää (Finland, HQ), Shanghai China (opened 2002), Germany (Demag/SWF Krantechnik heritage), USA (R&M Materials Handling, Springfield OH area), plus facilities via subsidiaries.
- **India:** Modern crane factory in **Pune (Pinjar/Pune-Saswad Road, Maharashtra)** — opened to serve the growing Asian market; also an Engineering Knowledge Center in Pune (Block 5/C, 209 Pune-Saswad Road, Pune 412308). Indian entity operates as **Konecranes and Demag Private Limited**.
- **Port cranes in India:** licensing/manufacturing arrangement with **Larsen & Toubro (L&T)** for making and distributing Konecranes port cranes in India.
- **Subsidiaries/brands:** Demag Cranes & Components, Donati Sollevamenti, Eurofactory, MHE-Demag, Morris Material Handling, R&M Materials Handling, Saudi Cranes & Steel Works Factory, SWF Krantechnik, Verlinde, Kocks Kranbau (acquired 2024), Peinemann (Rotterdam, acquired Dec 2024).

## 6. Export / Geographic Markets

## Threat to SVMH

Konecranes is a **Tier-1 global incumbent** and the single most formidable competitor in this landscape. Direct threat vectors relevant to SVMH:

1. **Local manufacturing presence in India (Pune)** + the **L&T port-crane partnership** means Konecranes competes on Indian soil for the same automotive/steel/power/cement customers SVMH targets — with local production, not just imports.
2. **Product overlap** is near-total on SVMH's core (EOT/overhead cranes, wire rope hoists, components/spares, AMC). Konecranes differentiates on automation, smart features (Sway Control, TRUCONNECT remote monitoring) and a global service network SVMH cannot replicate.
3. **Digital dominance:** localized Drupal site, IoT platform, spare-parts e-store, rich video/case-study content, strong social presence (LinkedIn/Instagram/YouTube). This is exactly the gap SVMH is trying to close — Konecranes sets the benchmark to study for the new SVMH website (clear industry landing pages, product series naming, case studies, service programs, downloadable brochures).
4. **Scale:** the India entity alone (~INR 544 Cr) dwarfs SVMH (~INR 13.6 Cr) ~40x. SVMH cannot win head-to-head on scale or brand; its realistic wedge is **price, responsiveness, local relationships, customization, and faster AMC for mid-market/regional buyers** — the segment where Konecranes' premium positioning and overhead are less competitive.

**Website takeaways for SVMH:** emulate Konecranes' industry-first navigation, series-based product pages, published case studies, service-program framing (preventive maintenance/modernization/parts), and downloadable spec brochures — while positioning SVMH as the agile, cost-effective, made-in-India local alternative.
