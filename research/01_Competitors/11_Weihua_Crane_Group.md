# Competitor Profile — Weihua Crane Group (Weihua Group Co., Ltd.)

**Date accessed:** 2026-07-05
**Researched for:** S.V. Material Handling System Pvt Ltd (SVMH), Bengaluru
**Primary sites:** https://www.globalweihua.com · https://www.weihuacraneglobal.com · https://www.henanweihua.com · https://www.weihuacranegroup.com
**Verdict:** Tier-1 global crane giant. Not a peer of SVMH — it is the aspirational benchmark and a direct threat in SVMH's core Indian metallurgical/EOT segment.

---

## 1. Company Overview

Weihua Group Co., Ltd. is one of the world's largest crane manufacturers, headquartered in Changyuan City, Henan Province, China. Founded in June 1988 by Han Xianbao as the "Xinxiang Weihua Crane Factory" — a six-employee handicraft workshop on less than 0.2 hectare — it has grown into a large diversified industrial group whose core business is lifting/material-handling equipment, with secondary sectors in port machinery, prefabricated construction (Weihua Construction Engineering Group), modern agriculture, intelligent parking, and special-purpose robots.

Weihua is the domestic market leader in China: single-girder and double-girder crane production/sales have ranked **No. 1 in China for seven consecutive years**, and electric hoists **No. 2 for seven consecutive years**. It is designated a **National Manufacturing Single Champion Demonstration Enterprise** and consistently appears on China's Top 500 Private Enterprises list (ranked 469th in 2025). Current chairman: Han Hong'an. The English-facing web presence is fragmented across many domains/microsites (globalweihua.com is a hook-focused microsite; weihuacraneglobal.com is the main global product/marketing site; henanweihua.com and weihuacranegroup.com are corporate hubs).

> "Weihua Group Co., Ltd. was founded in 1988 and is headquartered in Changyuan City, Henan Province. It is a large modern enterprise group primarily engaged in crane manufacturing. In 2024, its sales revenue reached 28.916 billion yuan." — Baidu Baike (Confidence: High)

**Slogan / positioning:** "Weihua, Making the World Easier" — "World leading crane manufacturer."

## 4. Product Categories

Broadest product line in the industry — 9–10 series, 200+ types:

- **Overhead (bridge/EOT) cranes:** single-girder, double-girder, hoist overhead, explosion-proof, grab, magnet, foundry, manual, metallurgical/ladle cranes
- **Gantry cranes:** single-girder, double-girder, semi-gantry, shipbuilding gantry, mini gantry
- **Hoists:** electric chain hoist, electric wire-rope hoist, anti-explosion, metallurgy, European-type, electric winch
- **Jib cranes:** foot-mounted, wall-mounted, column, traveling, mobile
- **Port / container cranes:** RMG, RTG, STS (ship-to-shore), portal, luffing jib, container carrier/stacker, wheel-type rail-laying crane
- **Crane components/spares** (directly overlaps SVMH): forged hooks (5t–3000t range), wheel assemblies, couplings, pulleys/sheaves, rope drums, motors, gearboxes/reducers, brakes, rope guides, control panels, pendant push buttons, limit switches, DSL-type systems
- **Other/diversified:** lifting platforms, electric trolleys, conveying equipment, multi-function cranes, PC (precast concrete) complete equipment lines, intelligent parking systems, AI cranes ("AICrane"), heavy-duty AGV handling robots (up to 300t)

## 6. Manufacturing Capabilities

- 3 modern production/manufacturing bases + 7 R&D centers (Changyuan HQ, Beijing, Shanghai, plus Jiangsu high-end port-equipment base under construction from 2023).
- ~1,700 sets of manufacturing and testing facilities (Henan Weihua unit).
- Intelligent/smart manufacturing: automated production cell lines, automatic robotic painting lines, automatic welding robots, automated gear cleaning/packaging lines; 5G fully-connected factory (Henan Province designation).
- Design per **FEM standards**; products meet ISO 4306/4309.
- Crane capacity range: from small workshop units up to **2,000-ton gantry cranes** (delivered 2022 to China's largest offshore wind mother port); 400-meter lifting-height gantry crane set a world record (2009); 250-ton ladle cranes and 320t ladle cranes in the metallurgy line.
- R&D spend: 4–6% of sales revenue for 20 consecutive years; CNAS-accredited testing center; proprietary anti-sway/anti-swing automatic positioning control system (appraised "internationally leading").

## Threat to SVMH

**High and rising in SVMH's home market.** Weihua is roughly 200x SVMH's size (RMB ~28.9 bn ≈ ₹33,000+ Cr vs SVMH's ~₹13.6 Cr) and is *actively targeting India*: it maintains an India office, delivered a metallurgical overhead crane project and a 250-ton ladle crane to the Indian market in 2026, and is running India-specific SEO content that speaks fluently to SVMH's exact standards vocabulary (IS 807, IS 3177, IS 4137, Factories Act 1948, FEM, M1–M8 duty classes). That content directly competes for the Google searches SVMH's own weak site should be capturing.

Where Weihua threatens SVMH most: heavy metallurgical/ladle/foundry cranes, large-tonnage double-girder EOT, and the crane-components/spares business (forged hooks, gearboxes, drums, sheaves, DSL/electrics) — all core SVMH lines — where Weihua can undercut on price at volume and outmatch on certifications (CE/SGS/BV) and automation features (anti-sway, AI cranes).

Where SVMH can still win: local manufacture and BIS/IS-native compliance without import lead times or duties; fast on-site AMC/service and emergency repair proximity; relationships and customization for small/medium Indian buyers who don't need 2,000-ton capability; and lower total cost for standard sub-50-ton EOT/gantry/jib cranes where a Chinese import's logistics, spares lead time, and after-sales gap are real disadvantages.

**Website implication:** Weihua sets the bar for what a modern crane website looks like — multi-language, deep product taxonomy (200+ SKUs), rich case-study/video library, downloadable catalogs, prominent certifications, and aggressive technical-blog SEO. SVMH's new site should match the structure (clear product tree, certifications wall, case studies, downloadable spec sheets, IS-standard technical content) while leaning hard on the "Made-in-India, BIS-compliant, local service & AMC, fast spares" differentiators Weihua structurally cannot match.
