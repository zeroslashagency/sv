## 1. Company Overview

GH Cranes & Components is a Spanish, family-owned industrial group founded in **1958** in Olaberria (Gipuzkoa, Basque Country) by four brothers — Alejandro, Jesús, Marcial, and Vicente Guerra. It is today one of the largest integrated (self-sufficient) manufacturers of hoists, overhead/bridge cranes, gantry cranes, and crane components in the world, now run with the third generation of the founding family involved. CEO of the GH Group is **José Antonio Guerra**.

The company positions itself around "intelligent lifting solutions" and proprietary technology, marketing the concept of the "intelligent crane" and "innovation is not an extra." Corporate values are branded as "Commitment Raised to the Power of Five": Leadership, Work, Innovation, Service, Honesty.

- **Presence:** 5 continents, ~70 countries (commercial presence historically cited as 65 countries).
- **Installed base:** 125,000–130,000+ cranes installed worldwide (marketing figures range 115,000 to 130,000 depending on source/date; most recent corporate pages say "more than 130,000" / "more than 125,000").
- **Headcount:** More than 1,000 employees group-wide.
- **International revenue share:** 78% of revenue from international markets (High confidence — corporate blog).

**Confidence: High** for founding/ownership/scale; the exact installed-base and country counts drift across sources so treat as approximate marketing figures.

## 3. Product Categories

From the global product menu (ghcranes.com/en/our-products/):

- Overhead (bridge) cranes — box girder and standard profile; single & double girder
- Gantry cranes (full and semi-gantry)
- Industrial jib cranes and cantilever cranes
- Marine jib cranes
- Automotive gantry cranes (industrial and marine variants)
- Dry docks / dry docking systems (boatyards)
- Motorized boat trailers (self-propelled)

- End trucks / end carriages, wheel heads, bogies
- Transfer carts

Also markets **intelligent/smart cranes** (variable-speed drives on all movements, anti-sway, remote monitoring, 3D design & structural-calculation tooling) and **ATEX/IECEx/UL/CSA explosion-proof "Ex" solutions**.

**Confidence: High** (direct from official product pages and homepage).

## 5. Manufacturing Capabilities

GH emphasizes **self-sufficiency**: it controls the full chain from design and engineering through in-house production of hoists, components, structures, and complete cranes. Stated **weekly production capacity**:

- 90 standard bridge cranes
- 40 hoists / kits
- 2 gantry cranes
- 1 special crane
- 0.5 automotive gantry cranes

Products designed to international standards **IEC, FEM, DIN, EN**. Strategic R&D alliances with European technology centres; pioneer in variable-speed drives across all crane motions; 3D design and structural calculation tooling.

**Confidence: High** (official About Us page).

## Threat to SVMH

GH is a **direct, high-tier threat** in SVMH's core market and product lines:

1. **On the ground in India.** GH Cranes India Pvt Ltd (Pune) means GH competes for the same automotive, steel, foundry, and power customers SVMH targets in western/southern India — with a global brand, deeper engineering, and full ISO/CE/ATEX credentials.
2. **Product overlap is near-total** (EOT single/double girder, gantry, jib, hoists, components/spares, process/foundry/steel cranes) but GH backs it with proprietary "intelligent crane" tech, variable-speed drives, and explosion-proof Ex ranges SVMH does not visibly offer.
3. **Digital dominance.** GH runs a modern, multilingual, content-rich website (blog, video library, downloadable catalogs, case studies, certificate PDFs) — exactly the credibility signals SVMH lacks while leaning on IndiaMART/ExportersIndia. This is the clearest, most actionable gap for SVMH's new website: publish catalogs, case studies, certifications, and project videos as GH does.
4. **Credibility gap.** GH's blue-chip client wall (Audi, VW, ArcelorMittal, Airbus, Vestas, Iberdrola) and 67-year, 5-continent story dwarf SVMH's ~Rs 13.6 Cr, 2006-founded regional profile in any head-to-head RFQ where the buyer researches online.

**Takeaway for the SVMH site:** match GH's information architecture (Products → Industries → Certifications → Case Studies → Downloads → Videos) and lead with verifiable proof (IS 807 / IS 3177 / FEM 9.511 compliance, ISO 9001, project photos, AMC track record) to close the online-credibility gap where GH currently wins by default.
