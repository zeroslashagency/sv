# Competitor Profile — Eurocrane (China) Co., Ltd.

**Research date:** 2026-07-05
**Analyst context:** Competitive landscape study for S.V. Material Handling Systems Pvt Ltd (SVMH), Bengaluru.
**Primary website:** https://www.eurocranegroup.com (global/export site) | https://www.eurocrane.com.cn (China corporate)
**Stock listing:** Shanghai Stock Exchange, ticker **603966 (SHA/SHSE)**, listed Jan 2017.

> One-line summary: Eurocrane is a large, publicly-listed (~US$375M+ revenue) Chinese crane and intelligent material-handling manufacturer based in Suzhou, exporting to 50+ countries, owner of the German VOITH crane brand. It operates in a completely different weight class from SVMH but competes directly for export/OEM crane business and on digital sophistication.

## 1. Company Overview

Eurocrane (China) Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer and service provider of cranes and material-handling products, focused on the R&D, design, manufacturing, installation and lifecycle service of "high-end" and intelligent lifting equipment. The company positions itself around "East-West Intelligence Serving The World," combining Chinese manufacturing scale with acquired European (VOITH) engineering.

## 3. Product Categories

Very broad portfolio — spans standard cranes, components/kits, and specialty/intelligent systems:

- **Crane Kits (modular):** suspension, single/double girder overhead kits (with hoist or winch), single/double girder gantry kits
- **Overhead Cranes:** single girder, single girder with dual hoists, double girder (with hoist / dual hoist / winch / winch + auxiliary hoist)
- **Gantry Cranes:** single girder, double girder, double girder with winch, semi-gantry
- **Industrial / Process Cranes:** casting (ladle) crane, paper mill crane, tank house crane
- **Explosion-Proof Cranes:** Ex hoists and Ex cranes
- **Clean Room Cranes & Hoists**
- **Suspension Cranes:** single girder
- **Light Crane Systems:** UKA monorail system, UKA single girder crane
- **Jib Cranes:** column-mounted, wall-mounted, mobile
- **Hoists:** electric chain, manual chain block, electric wire rope, double girder hoist, electric belt hoist, plus **Europe-produced** wire rope & chain hoists (VOITH-derived)
- **Winches:** open winch, open winch with trolley
- **Aerial Work Platforms:** scissor lift (incl. crawler type), articulating boom lift
- **Crane Parts / Components:** motors, gearboxes, brakes, end carriages/end trucks, trolleys, rails, cabs, cable reels, wire rope, hoist chain, buffers, hoist drums, cables, pendants, remote controls, frequency inverters, limit switches, electric cabinets
- **Intelligent systems:** automated/intelligent warehouse systems, WMS/WCS storage management, software control + hardware execution, real-time data collection, sensor/ground-imaging systems

Capacity range advertised: up to **600 t** (EW winch crane), gantry up to **300 t**, double-girder overhead up to **100 t** (per catalog titles); a flagship **400T/50t** transformer-manufacturing overhead crane is marketed.

*Source: eurocranegroup.com product menu + download page. Confidence: High.*

## Threat to SVMH

**Scale asymmetry (context, not head-to-head):** Eurocrane is ~US$375M revenue / ~1,900 employees / publicly listed — roughly **25x the size of SVMH (~Rs 13.6 Cr / ~US$1.6M FY24)**. They do not compete in the same tier for domestic Indian AMC/fabrication work.

**Where they DO threaten SVMH:**
1. **Export/OEM crane business & imports into India.** Chinese majors like Eurocrane price aggressively on standard EOT/gantry/hoist kits and components (DSL, gearboxes, hoists, end carriages) — the exact spares/components SVMH sells. Indian buyers sourcing on IndiaMART/ExportersIndia (SVMH's own channels) may compare against Eurocrane imports.
2. **Digital sophistication gap.** Eurocrane's website (SEO, gated catalogs, 360° tour, video library, multi-channel social, live chat) is a benchmark of what a modern crane-maker web presence looks like. **This is directly relevant to SVMH's goal of building a superior website** — Eurocrane shows the ceiling: rich product taxonomy, downloadable manuals, case studies, application pages, and strong technical-blog content.
3. **"European technology" positioning (VOITH).** Their East-meets-West narrative + brand-name customer logos (BMW, Siemens, ABB) is a credibility play SVMH cannot match on scale but can counter locally with proximity, faster service, IS-standard compliance, and personal AMC relationships.

**Takeaways for SVMH's website:** adopt a clear product taxonomy, publish downloadable spec sheets/catalogs, add real case studies with tonnage/industry, an applications-by-industry section, video content, and structured SEO — matching Eurocrane's content depth at a local/regional scale while emphasizing India-specific standards (IS 807/3177/FEM 9.511), MSME agility, and responsive after-sales/AMC.
