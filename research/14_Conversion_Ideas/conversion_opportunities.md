# Conversion Opportunity Report (SVMH Phase 1)

CRO opportunities — how to turn SVMH's future site into a lead engine that out-converts competitors. Grounded in the website audits, customer intelligence (buyer journey + personas), and SEO research in this repo (accessed 2026-07-05).

> **Core thesis:** Competitors lose conversions in three predictable ways — (1) they make buyers leave the product page to enquire, (2) they hide price and specs, (3) their heroes dilute the CTA. SVMH's conversion strategy is to remove friction at the exact moment of buyer intent, mirror the real IS 3177 RFQ mental model, and lead with proof.

## RFQ flow design (recommended)
1. **Entry:** on-page "Request a Quote" button (product pre-selected) or WhatsApp.
2. **Qualify (mirrors IS 3177):** crane type → capacity → span → lift height → duty/usage → indoor/outdoor/environment.
3. **Contact:** name, company, phone, email, city (minimal, after value shown).
4. **Confirm + fast-track:** instant WhatsApp/callback option; "typical response within X hours."
5. **Route:** hot/spec'd leads → sales; incomplete → nurture with buyer guide / spec-sheet email.

*This doubles as buyer education and lead qualification, and matches how buyers actually define requirements (per `05_Customer_Research`).*

## Trust signals to deploy (in priority order)
1. Industry-tagged reference installs + FAT/factory video (biggest trust lever per customer intel).
2. Certifications: ISO 9001, IS 807/3177 compliance declaration, GST, MSME (+ downloadable).
3. Years in business / installs count / capacity stats.
4. Named client logos (where permitted) + case studies.
5. Local service coverage map + guaranteed response time.
6. Spares stocking policy (the maintenance-engineer gatekeeper decides on this).
